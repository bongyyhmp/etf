## Bull case / bear case

- **Bull:** Analysts and coverage cite surging AI semiconductor revenue (fiscal Q4 guide of about $21.7B, up roughly 236%), record free cash flow, the $63B Anthropic-linked financing vehicle and Google's new TPU generation; most analysts rate it Buy with average targets above $500 per TradingKey, and the Motley Fool puts the forward P/E near 15 after a 40% fall from the record.
- **Bear:** William Blair's downgrade flags valuation, and reports cite heavy customer concentration, up to ~$43B of financing backstop exposure tied to Anthropic, gross margin guided down to about 73%, VMware transition friction and post-midterm data-center restrictions; even after the Nov 9 jump the stock remains far below its June record.
