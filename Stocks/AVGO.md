## Bull case / bear case

- **Bull:** Goldman Sachs names Broadcom a top pick at about 13x a no-growth 2028 baseline and GuruFocus sees it about 22% below its GF Value; analysts also cite surging AI semiconductor revenue (fiscal Q4 guide ~$21.7B, up ~236%), the $1B Anthropic share purchase and Google's new TPUs, and the stock rose for a third day on Nov 11 as CoreWeave's results revived AI spending hopes.
