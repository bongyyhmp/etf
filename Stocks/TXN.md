## Bull case / bear case

- **Bull:** Cantor Fitzgerald upgraded to Overweight ($385) on Nov. 23, citing an extended analog mid-cycle, TI's supply capacity and 2028 EPS potentially above $15, and JPMorgan (Overweight) lifted its target to $365; supporters also cite AI data-center and industrial demand, an operating margin that rebounded to 47.6% in fiscal Q3 (per Trefis), the planned Silicon Labs deal and the stock's rebound of about 4% on Nov. 25 (Finexus, TradingKey).
- **Bear:** Critics point to a valuation near 35x-40x trailing earnings (up from about 24x in 2021 while EPS is slightly lower, per Trefis), heavy fab capex weighing on free cash flow (a risk Cantor also flags), added acquisition debt, flat sequential Q4 guidance, slow auto and industrial demand, Asian price competition and Goldman's Sell ($250); [[ADI]]'s cautious outlook hit analog sentiment, new USTR Section 301 findings raise tariff risk (Finexus), and the stock has swung sharply (+11.8% on Nov. 23, a 7.6%-11.3% drop on Nov. 24, about -5% on the morning of Nov. 25 before a late rebound).
