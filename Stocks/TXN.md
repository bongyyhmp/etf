## Bull case / bear case

- **Bull:** Analysts cite an analog-chip recovery, AI data-center and industrial demand, improving margins and free cash flow once 300mm fab spending eases, the planned Silicon Labs acquisition and new agentic-AI and factory-networking software tools; JPMorgan (Overweight) lifted its target to $365, TradingKey puts the past-month average target near $326, Trefis notes the operating margin rebounded to 47.6% in fiscal Q3 2026 from 34.0% a year earlier, and the stock led [[ADI]] over the past year (95% total return vs. 69%).
- **Bear:** Critics point to a valuation near 38x-40x trailing earnings (up from about 24x in 2021 while EPS is slightly lower, per Trefis), heavy fab capex weighing on free cash flow, added acquisition debt, flat sequential Q4 guidance, slow auto and industrial demand, Asian price competition and institutional selling; Goldman Sachs rates it Sell ($250), and new U.S. AI-chip export rules helped drive a 7.6% drop on Nov 20 (SOX -10.7%) after declines of about 11% on Nov 5 and 3%-3.5% on Nov 13 and 16.
