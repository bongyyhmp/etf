## Bull case / bear case

- **Bull:** Analysts cite an analog-chip recovery, AI data-center and industrial demand, improving margins and free cash flow once fab spending eases, the planned Silicon Labs acquisition and new factory-networking and agentic-AI software tools shown at electronica (customers include ABB Robotics, Porsche and Schneider Electric); TradingKey puts the past-month average target near $326 (range $188-$400), Trefis notes its operating margin rebounded to 47.6% in fiscal Q3 2026 from 34.0% a year earlier, and the stock led [[ADI]] over the past year (95% total return vs. 69%).
- **Bear:** Critics point to a valuation near 38x-40x trailing earnings (up from about 24x in 2021 while EPS is slightly lower, per Trefis), heavy 300mm fab capex that weighs on free cash flow and margins, about $13.65B of debt plus new acquisition debt, flat sequential Q4 guidance, slow auto and industrial demand, Asian price competition and institutional and insider selling; Goldman Sachs rates it Sell ($250), new US AI-chip export rules and a weak Empire State reading weighed on Nov 16, and shares fell nearly 11% on Nov 5, 3.5% on Nov 13 and 3.2% on Nov 16 amid chip-sector selling.
