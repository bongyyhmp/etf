## Bull case / bear case

- **Bull:** Analysts (Goldman Sachs, Stifel, Truist, Bernstein, Susquehanna) cite AI data-center demand exceeding supply and the OpenAI/Meta GPU deals; Q3 data-center revenue nearly doubled to $8.5B, the ~$17B Q4 guide implies ~66% growth, and management sees 2027 revenue up more than 80%.
- **Bear:** Commentators note gross margin guided slightly lower (~55%), Gaming down 40%, PC-slump risk flagged by Intel through 2027, HBM supply limits, a BofA target cut on sector valuations and a muted post-earnings move, plus hyperscaler-capex, export-limit and customer-concentration risks.
