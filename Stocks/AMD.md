## Bull case / bear case

- **Bull:** Analysts (Oppenheimer's new Outperform at $600, Goldman Sachs, Stifel, Barclays, Bernstein) cite AI data-center demand exceeding supply, the OpenAI/Meta GPU deals and a Google Cloud TPU host-CPU role; Q3 data-center revenue nearly doubled to $8.5B, the ~$17B Q4 guide implies ~66% growth, and Lisa Su targets 2027 growth above 80%.
- **Bear:** Commentators note slightly lower gross-margin guidance (~55%), Gaming down 40%, an expected double-digit PC unit decline in 2027, HBM supply limits and a rich valuation (GuruFocus puts it about 56% above its GF Value) plus target trims from BofA and Goldman; the stock swung -17%, +11% and +13% over Nov 5-9.
