- **Bull:** Analysts (Oppenheimer Outperform at $600, Goldman Sachs top pick, Stifel, Barclays, Bernstein) cite AI data-center demand, MI450 ramp and OpenAI/Meta GPU deals; Q3 data-center revenue nearly doubled to $8.5B, the ~$17B Q4 guide implies ~66% growth, Lisa Su targets 2027 growth above 80%, and the stock gained another 5% on Nov 10 as money rotated from Nvidia.
