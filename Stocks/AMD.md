## Bull case / bear case

- **Bull:** Analysts (Goldman Sachs, Stifel, Barclays, Bernstein, Susquehanna) cite AI data-center demand exceeding supply and the OpenAI/Meta GPU deals; Q3 data-center revenue nearly doubled to $8.5B, the ~$17B Q4 guide implies ~66% growth, JPMorgan raised its target on Nov 6, and Lisa Su says AI demand is holding up.
- **Bear:** Commentators note slightly lower gross-margin guidance (~55%), Gaming down 40%, an expected double-digit PC unit decline in 2027, HBM supply limits, a richer valuation than Broadcom (about 42x forward per 24/7 Wall St.) and target trims from BofA and Goldman; the stock swung -17% and +11% on Nov 5-6 on data-center policy fears.
