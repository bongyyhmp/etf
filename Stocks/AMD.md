## Bull case / bear case

- **Bull:** Analysts (William Blair upgrade to Outperform, BofA Buy at $700 and top CPU pick, Oppenheimer Outperform at $600, Goldman Sachs top pick) cite AI data-center demand, the MI450 ramp, OpenAI/Meta GPU deals and a possible share opening from Nvidia's CEO turmoil; Q3 data-center revenue nearly doubled to $8.5B, the ~$17B Q4 guide implies ~66% growth, and UBS data show AMD holding about 28% of rising server CPU shipments.
- **Bear:** Goldman calls the valuation less compelling and TIKR notes about 37x forward earnings, above the five-year average; commentators also flag slightly lower gross-margin guidance (~55%), Gaming down 40%, an expected 2027 PC decline, HBM and supply limits that Lisa Su says cap how fast customers can shift orders, and sharp day-to-day swings since Nov 5, including a 7.6% drop on Nov 13 as the sector sold off and Michael Burry added bearish chip-ETF bets.
