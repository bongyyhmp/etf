## Bull case / bear case

- **Bull:** Analysts (JPMorgan, Citi, UBS; Bernstein Buy at EUR 2,500; BofA with a Street-high $2,818 target) cite AI-driven EUV demand; ASML says planned 2027-2028 supply won't fully cover demand, customer down payments rose to EUR 9.3B in Q3, 2026 sales guidance was raised to EUR 44-45B, buybacks continue at roughly EUR 440-560M a week, GuruFocus sees the latest U.S. chip rule as only a second-order issue for ASML, and shares rebounded in Amsterdam on Nov 18 as Nvidia's results eased AI-capex worries.
- **Bear:** GuruFocus rates it about fairly valued and 24/7 Wall St. puts its forward P/E (~24x) above equipment peers, saying bullish targets need everything to go right; repeated sharp declines (Nov 5, Nov 12, Nov 16 and a 5.3% Amsterdam drop on Nov 17) show sensitivity to AI and data-center jitters, and coverage flags reliance on chipmaker spending, tighter U.S. chip controls and proposed limits on DUV sales to China (about 20% of revenue).
