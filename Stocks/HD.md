## Bull case / bear case

- **Bull:** Supporters cite Q3 sales and comps ahead of forecasts, a 3% higher average ticket, online comps up about 13%, Pro outpacing DIY, full-year sales guidance at the top of its prior range and a continuity-minded CEO handoff to longtime CFO Richard McPhail (effective Nov 23); a four-session winning streak through a 2.1% gain to $361.26 on Nov 25 (JournalArta), an aging U.S. housing stock that the Motley Fool says should sustain repair demand, a $2.33 dividend (159th straight quarter), and Buy-type ratings that held through post-report trims, including UBS ($417), TD Cowen and Telsey (~$400-410), Guggenheim and Citi ($400) and Goldman ($371).
- **Bear:** Skeptics point to a ~2.7% post-report drop and target cuts from Goldman, UBS, Telsey, Mizuho, JPMorgan, Evercore ISI, RBC and Guggenheim (which cites higher input and fuel costs), a narrowed outlook implying a softer Q4 and adjusted EPS a bit below consensus, shrinking margins (gross 32.8% vs. 33.4%; operating margin outlook ~12.8%), still-falling transactions, a housing market chilled by ~7.3% mortgage rates and a likely December Fed hike after hot PCE data, new Section 301 tariff proposals that could lift import costs, SRS organic growth cut on roofing weakness, a ~22x forward P/E versus Lowe's ~17x, cautious calls from Stifel (Hold, $322) and Bernstein (Market Perform), and a recent ~$1M share sale by EVP Teresa Roseborough (Simply Wall St).
