## Bull case / bear case

- **Bull:** Supporters cite the Pro business (SRS comping above the company average; even the cautious Stifel calls HD's Pro platform stronger than Lowe's), 11% digital sales growth, share gains despite a weak housing market, hopes for housing-affordability policy after the midterms, reaffirmed fiscal 2026 guidance with 13 of 16 departments posting positive comps, and $400 targets from JPMorgan (Overweight) and Citi (Buy) (24/7 Wall St., TIKR, MarketBeat, Ad-hoc News, Zacks).
- **Bear:** Skeptics point to housing turnover near historic lows with no sign of a turn, falling customer transactions, SG&A growing faster than sales, a valuation (about 22.3x forward earnings, per Zacks) now slightly above its five-year median after a ~20% one-month rally, rising fuel and input costs, chart resistance near $358 (Schaeffer's), low odds that housing aid passes, Stifel's Nov 13 caution (softer second half, fiscal 2027-28 margin pressure, rivals pricing about 3% lower, a valuation near the top of its five-year range; Hold, $322 target) and Bernstein's Market Perform ($357, roughly at the price) (TIKR, 24/7 Wall St., MarketBeat, Zacks, Investing.com).
