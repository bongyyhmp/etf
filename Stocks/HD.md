## Bull case / bear case

- **Bull:** Supporters cite the Pro business (SRS comping above the company average; even the cautious Stifel calls HD's Pro platform stronger than Lowe's), 11% digital sales growth, a rising average ticket (TradingKey), share gains despite a weak housing market, hopes for housing-affordability policy after the midterms, reaffirmed fiscal 2026 guidance with 13 of 16 departments posting positive comps, rotation into retailers lifting the stock about 18% in 30 days (Stocks Today), and $400 targets from JPMorgan (Overweight) and Citi (Buy) (24/7 Wall St., TIKR, MarketBeat, Ad-hoc News, Zacks).
