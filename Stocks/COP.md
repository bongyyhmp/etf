## Bull case / bear case

- **Bull:** Coverage of the Nov 5 report cites a Q3 EPS and revenue beat, $4.0B of free cash flow, an 11% dividend increase, the 45% cash-return target, a reaffirmed $7B free-cash-flow inflection by 2029 (CEO Andy O'Brien: "2029 is not the endpoint") and targets of $174 (Jefferies), $175 (Susquehanna) and $185 (Wells Fargo, Overweight, trimmed Nov 6).
