## Bull case / bear case

- **Bull:** Coverage points to 14.5% revenue growth with margin expansion, electrophysiology up about 15%, the Exact Sciences diagnostics boost (with the ColoSense dispute settled), a run of heart-device approvals and trial wins (UltiSynq, Paraclose, SonicForce, Amulet 360) and the new Encantor TAVI study, the December Libre Duo launch and an expected post-spin growth lift of 50-100 basis points (Motley Fool, 24/7 Wall St.), JPMorgan's view that the nutrition spin could unlock value, Leerink's Nov 19 upgrade to Outperform, a forward P/E (~18-19x) below its five-year median (Zacks), a ~$104 support zone that preceded past rebounds (Trefis) and held again with a 2.5-3.5% gain to about $108 on Nov 25 (JournalArta, TradingKey), and an average target near $119 (TradingKey).
- **Bear:** Critics cite the cut to 2026 sales growth guidance on electrophysiology and diabetes care headwinds (RBC said it could weigh on 2027; Zacks rates it Hold), CGM growth slowing to 8% and shares down about 15-18% this year (24/7 Wall St., Trefis), operating margin down to 13.8% from 17.6% with Trefis warning a close below about $98 could signal further declines, a slight adjusted EPS miss versus Zacks consensus, Argus's downgrade on reimbursement and competition concerns, and ongoing infant formula litigation (Reuters, Zacks, Argus).
