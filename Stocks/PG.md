## Bull case / bear case

- **Bull:** Commentators cite 4% organic growth with all 10 categories growing, a core EPS beat ($2.00 vs. ~$1.89), maintained fiscal 2027 guidance ($6.89-7.11 core EPS), the Investor Day target of up to $10B in fiscal 2027-2030 productivity savings (Reuters), which Finimize says could help protect gross margins, 70 straight years of dividend increases (Motley Fool), a new 52-week high close of $173.02 on Nov 16 (Investing.com), a planned $15B of shareholder returns this fiscal year (24/7 Wall St.), and raised targets from Wells Fargo ($188, Overweight, Nov 18), Raymond James ($185) and Morgan Stanley ($185).
- **Bear:** Commentators flag a $1.4B after-tax commodity-cost hit for fiscal 2027 (about 11% of core EPS), margin compression (core operating margin down 80 bp), materially higher costs expected over the next two quarters, a valuation at the upper end of its historical P/E range (Ad-hoc News) with the price near the ~$171-174 average target and 11 analysts at Hold (24/7 Wall St.), an Investor Day built around cost cuts, AI and a 20-30% smaller supply workforce rather than reported new growth targets, with shares down about 1% during the event (Reuters), and a run of executive share sales, including by the CEO, the chief accounting officer, the CFO and the Grooming CEO.
