## Bull case / bear case

- **Bull:** Commentators cite 4% organic growth with all 10 categories growing, a core EPS beat ($2.00 vs. ~$1.89), maintained fiscal 2027 guidance ($6.89-7.11 core EPS), restructuring savings, 70 straight years of dividend increases (Motley Fool), defensive appeal that kept shares near record highs after the midterms, and Wells Fargo's $180 Overweight target (MarketBeat average about $171), with a Nov 19 Investor Day ahead to lay out long-term strategy.
- **Bear:** Commentators flag a $1.4B after-tax commodity-cost hit for fiscal 2027 (about 11% of core EPS), margin compression (core operating margin down 80 bp), revenue that Trefis called a miss (Ad-hoc News says it topped the LSEG estimate), materially higher costs expected over the next two quarters, a valuation slightly above its five-year average, and a run of executive share sales, including by the CEO, the chief accounting officer, the CFO (a 30,677-share sale on Nov 5 disclosed Nov 9) and the Grooming CEO, with shares trading just below MarketBeat's ~$171 average target.
