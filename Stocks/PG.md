## Bull case / bear case

- **Bull:** Commentators cite 4% organic growth with all 10 categories growing, a core EPS beat ($2.00 vs. ~$1.89), maintained fiscal 2027 guidance ($6.89-7.11 core EPS), restructuring savings, a long dividend growth record, defensive appeal that kept shares near record highs after the midterms, and Wells Fargo's $180 Overweight target (MarketBeat average about $171), with a Nov 19 Investor Day ahead to lay out long-term strategy.
- **Bear:** Commentators flag a $1.4B after-tax commodity-cost hit for fiscal 2027 (about 11% of core EPS), margin compression, a revenue miss, materially higher costs expected in fiscal Q2, and a run of executive share sales, including by the CEO, the chief accounting officer and the CFO (a 30,677-share sale on Nov 5 disclosed Nov 9), with shares trading just below MarketBeat's ~$171 average target.
