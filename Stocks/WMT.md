## Bull case / bear case

- **Bull:** Supporters point to a Q3 beat on revenue ($188.8B) and adjusted EPS ($0.65), adjusted operating income growth above guidance, a third raise to fiscal 2027 guidance, U.S. e-commerce up 23%, ads up 37%, membership fees up 17% and its biggest share gains in three years; post-report target raises from TD Cowen ($147), Telsey ($145), Roth ($143), Guggenheim ($140), Piper Sandler ($138) and Truist ($134), which argue e-commerce margins, ads and membership justify a premium; and its record of holding up when tech sells off (Guggenheim).
- **Bear:** Skeptics note U.S. comps slowed to 2.9% (vs. ~3.1% expected and 4.5% a year ago), the sales outlook trailed some forecasts and shares slipped about 1% after the report (Yahoo Finance); GAAP profit fell 19.5%; the top of the new EPS range only matches consensus at roughly 43x forward earnings, while Target and Dollar General grew comps faster at lower multiples; Guggenheim warns a risk-on return could cool demand for defensive retailers, tariff refunds must be lapped in fiscal 2028, drug pricing drags on comps, and the yield is under 1%.
