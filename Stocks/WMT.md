## Bull case / bear case

- **Bull:** Commentators cite a strong Q2 beat, growth in ads, membership (fee revenue up 17%) and marketplace income, AI shopping tools such as Sparky, a roughly $6B Symbotic robotics expansion, and pre-earnings support from JPMorgan (Overweight, target raised to $145 on Nov 9), UBS (earnings growth seen outpacing sales) and Bernstein (Buy, $142).
- **Bear:** Commentators flag a valuation of roughly 40-46x earnings against single-digit revenue growth, a sub-1% dividend yield, JPMorgan's U.S. comp estimate slightly below consensus (+2.7% vs +2.9%), doubts over membership monetization (Morgan Stanley survey), FTC formal orders on personalized pricing, pressure on lower-income shoppers and margin mix ahead of the holidays, and insider share sales.
