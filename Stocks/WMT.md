## Bull case / bear case

- **Bull:** Commentators cite a strong Q2 beat, growth in ads, membership (fee revenue up 17%) and marketplace income, AI shopping tools such as Sparky and a roughly $6B Symbotic robotics expansion, with pre-earnings target raises from UBS ($149, Buy), JPMorgan ($145, Overweight), BofA ($139, Buy), BMO ($136, Outperform), Mizuho ($135, Outperform) and Wells Fargo ($135, Overweight, Nov 13), plus Bernstein (Buy, $142) and BTIG (Buy, $140); per Koyfin data cited by Stocktwits, 38 of 43 analysts rate it Buy or Strong Buy.
- **Bear:** Commentators flag a valuation of roughly 38-46x earnings against single-digit revenue growth (a Nov 13 Motley Fool column notes about 38x forward earnings versus a ~27x five-year average), a sub-1% dividend yield, an average analyst target (about $129, per Koyfin) roughly at the share price, comp estimates near or below consensus (JPMorgan +2.7%) and BMO's cut to its fiscal 2028 comp forecast on drug-pricing headwinds, doubts over membership monetization (Morgan Stanley survey), FTC formal orders on personalized pricing, pressure on lower-income shoppers, more than $2B of added fuel-related costs with diesel a risk into 2027 (24/7 Wall St., which notes one Sell rating among 43 analysts), and insider share sales.
