## Bull case / bear case

- **Bull:** Bulls cite the Q3 beat (medical loss ratio 88.4%), 2026 guidance raised to at least $20.50, an expanded 50-million-share buyback, added institutional buying such as Fisher Asset Management's 19% larger Q3 stake, Wells Fargo's view that the fundamental recovery remains on track, Bernstein's expectation of no Medicare Advantage cuts after the midterms, easing medical costs reported across insurers such as Humana, softer near-term Medicaid work-rule enforcement, a P/E (about 22.7x) below the industry average per Simply Wall St, and an average analyst target near $464 (20 Buy, 8 Hold, per Blockonomi).
- **Bear:** Wells Fargo, Raymond James and others flag political risk, with a Democratic Congress expected to bring tougher PBM oversight (pressure on Optum Rx) and Sen. Warren's push to break up vertically integrated insurers; CMS's proposed CRUSH rule would widen Medicare Advantage RADV audits (an analyst cited by Seeking Alpha sees it as potentially materially negative for MA insurers, though likely litigated), sending shares down 5.4% on Nov 17, JPMorgan projects fewer UnitedHealth members in 4-star-plus MA plans for 2027, Ad-hoc News flags elevated commercial and Medicaid costs and Medicare legal actions, and several firms trimmed targets after Q3.
