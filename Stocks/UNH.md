## Bull case / bear case

- **Bull:** Bulls cite the Q3 beat (medical loss ratio 88.4%), 2026 guidance raised to at least $20.50, an expanded 50-million-share buyback, added institutional buying such as Fisher Asset Management's 19% larger Q3 stake, Wells Fargo's view that the fundamental recovery remains on track, Bernstein's expectation of no Medicare Advantage cuts after the midterms, easing medical costs reported across insurers such as Humana, a P/E (about 22.7x) below the industry average per Simply Wall St, and continued deal appetite (a reported, rejected $28/share bid for Surgery Partners).
- **Bear:** Wells Fargo, Raymond James and others flag political risk, with a Democratic Congress expected to bring tougher PBM oversight (pressure on Optum Rx) and Sen. Warren's call to break up "Big Medicine"; JPMorgan projects fewer UnitedHealth members in 4-star-plus Medicare Advantage plans for 2027, TradingKey tied the Nov 5 drop to medical-cost trends and payment scrutiny, Ad-hoc News flags elevated commercial and Medicaid costs and Medicare legal actions, and several firms trimmed targets after Q3.
