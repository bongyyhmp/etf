## Bull case / bear case

- **Bull:** Bulls cite the Q3 beat (medical loss ratio 88.4%), 2026 guidance raised to at least $20.50, an expanded 50-million-share buyback, Fisher Asset Management's 19% larger Q3 stake, Wells Fargo's view that the fundamental recovery remains on track, easing medical costs reported across insurers, an average analyst target near $464-470 (Blockonomi, TradingKey) far above the ~$330 price, Oppenheimer's view that the selloff is an overreaction because the CRUSH audit rule is only proposed, likely to be litigated and could be priced into future bids, and Raymond James's view that Medicare for All fears may be overstated since the proposals stop well short of ending private insurance.
- **Bear:** Critics point to a roughly 20% three-session drop to $330.48 (Nov 17-19) as CMS's proposed CRUSH rule would extrapolate Medicare Advantage audit findings back to 2018 while the company has not sized its exposure (Tickeron) and Mizuho calls the impact uncertain, a renewed Medicare for All push from Sen. Sanders, who is set to chair the Senate HELP Committee (Forbes), Sen. Warren's call to break up vertically integrated insurers, expected tougher PBM oversight from a Democratic Congress (Wells Fargo, Raymond James), JPMorgan's projection of fewer members in 4-star-plus MA plans for 2027, and elevated commercial and Medicaid costs (Ad-hoc News).
