## Bull case / bear case

- **Bull:** Bulls cite the Q3 beat, 2026 EPS guidance of at least $20.50 and an expanded buyback; TD Cowen upgraded to Buy on Nov 20 ($455 target), Oppenheimer calls the CRUSH selloff an overreaction to a proposal likely to be litigated, Raymond James thinks Medicare for All fears are overstated, JPMorgan says insurers have time to adapt to the proposed star-ratings changes (still not final), and the average target (~$464-470) sits well above the ~$336 price.
- **Bear:** Critics point to two CMS proposals in a week: the CRUSH audit rule extrapolating Medicare Advantage findings back to 2018 (exposure unsized; Mizuho calls the impact uncertain) and a star-ratings overhaul that CMS estimates would cut MA payments by about $107B over 2028-2037, with TD Cowen naming UnitedHealth among the most exposed; they also cite Sen. Sanders' Medicare for All push, tougher PBM oversight expected from a Democratic Congress (Wells Fargo, Raymond James), fewer members in 4-star-plus plans for 2027 (JPMorgan) and elevated commercial and Medicaid costs.
