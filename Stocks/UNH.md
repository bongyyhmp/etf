## Bull case / bear case

- **Bull:** Bulls cite the Q3 beat (medical loss ratio 88.4%), 2026 guidance raised to at least $20.50, an expanded 50-million-share buyback, added institutional buying such as Fisher Asset Management's 19% larger Q3 stake, Wells Fargo's view that the fundamental recovery remains on track, Bernstein's expectation of no Medicare Advantage cuts after the midterms, easing medical costs reported across insurers such as Humana, softer near-term Medicaid work-rule enforcement, a P/E (about 22.7x) below the industry average per Simply Wall St, an average analyst target near $464-470 (20 Buy, 8 Hold, per Blockonomi; TradingKey ~$470), and Oppenheimer's Nov 18 view that the managed-care selloff is an overreaction since the CRUSH audit rule is only proposed and likely to face court challenges.
- **Bear:** Wells Fargo, Raymond James and others flag political risk, with a Democratic Congress expected to bring tougher PBM oversight (pressure on Optum Rx) and Sen. Warren's push to break up vertically integrated insurers; CMS's proposed CRUSH rule would widen Medicare Advantage RADV audits (an analyst cited by Seeking Alpha sees it as potentially materially negative for MA insurers, though likely litigated), sending shares down 5.4% on Nov 17 and another 10.2% on Nov 18 while the company has not quantified its exposure (Tickeron), JPMorgan projects fewer UnitedHealth members in 4-star-plus MA plans for 2027, Ad-hoc News flags elevated commercial and Medicaid costs and Medicare legal actions, and several firms trimmed targets after Q3.
