## Bull case / bear case

- **Bull:** Barclays (Overweight, $450, citing 24/7 camera-only robotaxi service) and ARK Invest cite SpaceX-combination optionality and robotaxi potential; Musk said on Nov 18 the companies would "probably" be better together and that a merger alone should not earn him extra stock, and TD Securities sees ~$100B of index-fund buying if a combined firm keeps Tesla's S&P 500 seat; supporters also point to a Q3 delivery beat, robotaxi expansion (public Cybercab rides in Dallas from Nov 19, a record 200 Cybercab registrations in a day, a roughly doubled Miami area), Cybercab orders at $29,990, Musk's pledge to keep raising Shanghai output, a planned ~€3B Berlin Cybercab plant (Reuters) and an average analyst target near $392 (TradingKey).
- **Bear:** Reports cite a 56% drop in Q3 operating income, a 3.5% operating margin (Trefis), a 28.9% month-on-month fall in October China-made sales, sparse robotaxi disclosure (GuruFocus) and eight NHTSA-logged Cybercab crashes (the driverless ones all below 5 mph, per Electrek via ad-hoc-news), a P/E above 400 and a Hold consensus; a SpaceX deal faces China, governance and Washington hurdles, Musk himself said it is not certain and Polymarket odds of a 2026 announcement fell to about 16%, Barclays sees SpaceX buying Tesla and leaving Tesla holders a minority, camera-only autonomy remains debated (Investing.com), and the Berlin Cybercab plant would not produce before 2029.
