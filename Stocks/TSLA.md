## Bull case / bear case

- **Bull:** Barclays and ARK Invest cite SpaceX-combination optionality and robotaxi potential, and Bloomberg reported on Nov 12 that merger talks are advancing (TD Securities sees ~$100B of index-fund buying if the combined firm keeps Tesla's S&P 500 seat); supporters also point to a Q3 delivery beat, a new robotaxi city (San Antonio), a planned ~€3B Berlin Cybercab plant (Reuters), extended Cybertruck output on strong orders, a $10.1B Texas solar plant and an average analyst target near $392 (TradingKey); the stock had its best week since May through Nov 13.
- **Bear:** Reports cite a 56% drop in Q3 operating income, a 3.5% operating margin (Trefis), a 28.9% month-on-month fall in October China-made sales, sparse robotaxi disclosure (GuruFocus) and a P/E above 400; a SpaceX deal faces China, governance and Washington hurdles, and Barclays sees SpaceX buying Tesla, leaving Tesla holders a minority, which GuruFocus suggested weighed on shares as they fell about 3.4% on Nov 16 (TradingKey); the stock is down about 13% this year (Yahoo Finance), and the Berlin Cybercab plant would not produce before 2029.
