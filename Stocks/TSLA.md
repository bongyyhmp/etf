## Bull case / bear case

- **Bull:** Barclays (Overweight, $450, citing 24/7 camera-only robotaxi service) and ARK Invest cite SpaceX-combination optionality and robotaxi potential, and Bloomberg reported on Nov 12 that merger talks are advancing (TD Securities sees ~$100B of index-fund buying if the combined firm keeps Tesla's S&P 500 seat); supporters also point to a Q3 delivery beat, robotaxi expansion (San Antonio launch, a roughly doubled Miami service area), Cybercab orders opening at $29,990 with Musk signaling a lower subscription fee, a planned ~€3B Berlin Cybercab plant (Reuters), extended Cybertruck output on strong orders and an average analyst target near $392 (TradingKey).
- **Bear:** Reports cite a 56% drop in Q3 operating income, a 3.5% operating margin (Trefis), a 28.9% month-on-month fall in October China-made sales, sparse robotaxi disclosure (GuruFocus) and eight NHTSA-logged Cybercab crashes (the driverless ones all below 5 mph, per Electrek via ad-hoc-news), a P/E above 400 and a Hold consensus; a SpaceX deal faces China, governance and Washington hurdles, and Barclays sees SpaceX buying Tesla, leaving Tesla holders a minority, which GuruFocus suggested weighed on Monday's drop; camera-only autonomy remains debated (Investing.com), and the Berlin Cybercab plant would not produce before 2029.
