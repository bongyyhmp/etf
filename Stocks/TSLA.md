## Bull case / bear case

- **Bull:** Barclays (Overweight, $450) and ARK Invest cite robotaxi progress and SpaceX-combination optionality; HSBC, upgrading to Hold with a $359 target, values Tesla at $507 a share in a SpaceX takeover, and TD Securities sees ~$100B of index-fund buying if a combined firm keeps the S&P 500 seat; supporters also point to the FSD v15 rollout, a Q3 delivery beat, an 83.5% jump in October EU registrations (ACEA via 24/7 Wall St.), public Cybercab rides in Dallas, record Cybercab registrations, rising Shanghai output and a planned ~€3B Berlin Cybercab plant (Reuters), and the stock jumped 11.5% on Nov 23.
- **Bear:** Reports cite a 56% drop in Q3 operating income, a 3.5% operating margin (Trefis), a P/E above 400 and a Hold consensus (UBS stays Neutral at $430), with BYD outselling it in the EU this year; HSBC puts stand-alone value at just $212, Barclays sees a SpaceX deal leaving Tesla holders a minority, Musk calls a deal uncertain, TradingKey flags a 66% October drop in domestic China deliveries from Shanghai and an NHTSA audit of Cybercab's self-certification, robotaxi disclosure is sparse with eight NHTSA-logged Cybercab crashes (Electrek), and the stock swings sharply (down 7.6% on Nov 20, up 11.5% on Nov 23).
