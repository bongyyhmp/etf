## Bull case / bear case

- **Bull:** Fiscal Q4 beat with record revenue ($4.37B, +42%) and adjusted EPS ($4.06, +80%), ~74.5% adjusted gross margin, Communications up 81% and Industrial up 55%, while fiscal 2026 brought $15.2B of revenue (+38%) and $5.5B of free cash flow; management says most growth came from non-AI markets, and pre-report targets from Cantor ($575, Overweight), Roth ($505), Evercore ISI ($462) and Raymond James ($430) sit far above the post-report price.
- **Bear:** The fiscal Q1 guide ($4.4B, $3.82 adjusted EPS) came in roughly flat sequentially and below consensus per GuruFocus, and the stock fell 17.3% on Nov. 24 in what Investing.com called a sell-the-news move; GuruFocus, Trefis and Investing.com had flagged a rich valuation (roughly 42x-48x earnings) leaving it exposed to multiple compression, its high correlation with Nvidia ties it to AI-trade swings, and critics cite low-single-digit auto growth, inventory digestion and insider selling.
