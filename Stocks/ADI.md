## Bull case / bear case

- **Bull:** Analysts (Roth, initiated at Buy with a $505 target; Raymond James Outperform at $430, expecting a beat-and-raise on Nov 24; TD Cowen, $475) cite accelerating growth, with fiscal Q3 revenue up ~39%, Communications revenue up 84% on data-center demand, ~74% gross margins, Street Q4 forecasts near $4.32B (+40%) and AI tie-ups such as its Nvidia physical-AI work; Trefis credits EPS growth of about 85% since 2021 and a wider operating margin for its five-year outperformance of [[TXN]], and TradingKey's average target is near $470.
- **Bear:** GuruFocus, Trefis and Investing.com flag a rich valuation (roughly 42x-48x earnings vs. ~36.5x for peers) that leaves the stock exposed to multiple compression, as seen on Nov 20 when it fell about 7%-8% in a chip rout (SOX -10.7%) driven by AI-spending doubts and a reported SEC review of Nvidia; critics also cite three-year sales growth slightly below peers, low-single-digit auto growth, inventory digestion at industrial and auto customers, insider selling (director Ray Stata) and some recent cuts to Hold.
