## Bull case / bear case

- **Bull:** Analysts (Roth, initiated at Buy with a $505 target; TD Cowen, $475) cite accelerating growth, with fiscal Q3 revenue up ~39%, Communications revenue up 84% on data-center demand, ~74% gross margins, Street Q4 forecasts near $4.32B (+40%), and AI tie-ups such as its Nvidia physical-AI work and the just-closed Alif edge-AI deal; Trefis credits widening margins and EPS growth for its long-run outperformance (TradingKey average target ~$470).
- **Bear:** GuruFocus and Trefis flag a rich valuation (Trefis: about 42x earnings vs. ~36.5x for peers; TradingKey ~48x trailing), three-year sales growth slightly below the typical chipmaker, low-single-digit auto growth, insider selling (director Ray Stata's Nov 4 sale) and a possible AI-capex slowdown; the stock broke below its 200-day average on Nov 6, fell 3.7% on Nov 13 and 3.1% on Nov 16 (JKN cited ETF net selling) ahead of the Nov 24 report, and some recent ratings were cut to Hold.
