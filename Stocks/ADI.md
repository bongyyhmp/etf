## Bull case / bear case

- **Bull:** Analysts (TD Cowen, target $475) cite accelerating growth, with fiscal Q3 revenue up ~39%, ~40% operating margins, Street Q4 forecasts near $4.32B (+40%), and AI tie-ups such as its Nvidia physical-AI work and the just-closed Alif edge-AI deal; Trefis credits widening margins and EPS growth for its long-run outperformance, and the stock rose about 5% on Nov 9 in a broad analog rally.
- **Bear:** GuruFocus and Trefis flag a rich valuation (Trefis: about 42x earnings vs. ~36.5x for peers), three-year sales growth slightly below the typical chipmaker, low-single-digit auto growth, continued insider selling (director Ray Stata's Nov 4 sale) and a possible AI-capex slowdown; the stock broke below its 200-day average on Nov 6 and, even after the Nov 9 bounce, remains well under late-October levels.
