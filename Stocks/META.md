## Bull case / bear case

- **Bull:** Analysts (JPMorgan, which added Meta to its focus list with a $940 target, plus KeyBanc, Cantor, Guggenheim, BofA, Goldman, Pivotal) cite core ad strength, Q4 revenue guidance of $73B-$76B, above consensus, and early Muse AI traction (Citi sees $27B of Muse revenue by 2030); record bond order books and Moody's stable Aa3 rating show strong credit demand, and the stock sits about 18% below its September high (Motley Fool).
- **Bear:** Analysts (Needham, Scotiabank) cite dilution from equity raises and unproven Muse profitability; about $25B of new bonds this month, negative Q3 free cash flow, capex of $135B-$145B in 2026 and S&P's negative outlook point to rising leverage, which Investing.com linked to AI-debt worries in a ~2.4% drop on Nov 16; regulators keep pressing (an €805M Italian privacy fine on Nov 14, a £450,000 Ofcom fine, EU orders on WhatsApp AI access, Canada's Bill C-22), and executive turnover includes Dave Wehner's exit.
