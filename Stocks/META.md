## Bull case / bear case

- **Bull:** Analysts (JPMorgan, which added Meta to its focus list with a $940 target, plus KeyBanc, Cantor, Guggenheim, BofA, Goldman, Pivotal) cite core ad strength, Q4 revenue guidance above consensus and early Muse AI traction (Citi sees $27B of Muse revenue by 2030); record bond order books show strong credit demand.
- **Bear:** Analysts (Needham, Scotiabank) cite dilution from the equity raises and unproven Muse profitability; S&P's negative outlook, more than $20B of new bonds at higher coupons and capex near $140B in 2026 point to rising leverage, while TradingKey flags softer ad budgets and litigation; regulators are pressing too (EU orders on WhatsApp AI access, which Meta is appealing, and a £450,000 Ofcom fine on Nov 12), United Airlines is blocking AI booking agents (WSJ), and executive turnover includes strategy chief Dave Wehner's exit and infrastructure head Santosh Janardhan's 2027 handoff; shares fell ~2% on Nov 11 and ~0.8% on Nov 12.
