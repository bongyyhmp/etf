## Bull case / bear case

- **Bull:** Analysts (JPMorgan, which added Meta to its focus list with a $940 target, plus KeyBanc, Cantor, Guggenheim, BofA, Goldman, Pivotal) cite core ad strength, Q4 revenue guidance of $73B-$76B, above consensus, and early Muse AI traction (Citi sees $27B of Muse revenue by 2030); repeated large bond sales (including $15B on Nov 16) and Moody's stable Aa3 rating show strong credit demand, and the stock sits well below its September high (Motley Fool).
- **Bear:** Analysts (Needham, Scotiabank) cite dilution from equity raises (forward sales now cover 40M shares) and unproven Muse profitability; another $15B bond sale on Nov 16 on top of about $25B earlier this month, negative Q3 free cash flow, capex of $135B-$145B in 2026 and S&P's negative outlook point to rising leverage, which Investing.com linked to AI-debt worries; regulators keep pressing (an €805M Italian privacy fine, a £450,000 Ofcom fine, EU orders on WhatsApp AI access, Canada's Bill C-22), a shareholder suit has led Meta to pause the Zuckerberg share plan (Bloomberg), and shares fell for a third day on Nov 18 to about $616, roughly 22% below their record (24/7 Wall St.).
