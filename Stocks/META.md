## Bull case / bear case

- **Bull:** Analysts (Wells Fargo $995, Truist $1,000, UBS $815, JPMorgan at a reduced $775, Morgan Stanley Overweight at $720, plus KeyBanc, Cantor, Guggenheim, BofA and Goldman; average target near $848 per TradingKey) cite core ad strength (Q3 revenue +26%), above-consensus Q4 guidance of $73B-$76B, holiday ad demand and Advantage+ AI tools, and early Muse AI traction (10M+ users; possible restored Amazon access); Investing.com notes a modest ~21x trailing and ~17.6x 2027 P/E, and the shares rose 3.4% on Nov 25 while the Nasdaq fell.
- **Bear:** Analysts (Needham; Scotiabank at Hold, $650) cite dilution from a ~$97.5B equity plan, unproven Muse profitability, about $40B of bonds this month, negative Q3 free cash flow and 2026 capex of $135B-$145B (Wells Fargo sees ~$250B in 2027), with S&P on negative outlook and JPMorgan and Morgan Stanley cutting estimates on AI costs and dilution; regulators keep pressing (an €805M Italian privacy fine, Ofcom and EU actions), a Delaware suit has frozen the Zuckerberg share plan, the CFO sold about $5.7M of stock, and shares remain about 22% below the $777.59 September high.
