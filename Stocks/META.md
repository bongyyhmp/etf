## Bull case / bear case

- **Bull:** Analysts (JPMorgan, which added Meta to its focus list with a $940 target, plus KeyBanc, Cantor, Guggenheim, BofA, Goldman, Pivotal) cite core ad strength, Q4 revenue guidance above consensus and early Muse AI traction (Citi sees $27B of Muse revenue by 2030); record bond order books show strong credit demand, and shares held Monday's 5% jump on Nov 10.
- **Bear:** Analysts (Needham, Scotiabank) cite dilution from the equity raises and unproven Muse profitability; S&P's negative outlook, more than $20B of new bonds at higher coupons and capex near $140B in 2026 point to rising leverage, while TradingKey flags softer ad budgets and litigation, the EU is forcing open WhatsApp to rival AI assistants (Meta is appealing) and strategy chief Dave Wehner is departing.
