## Bull case / bear case

- **Bull:** Analysts (JPMorgan, which added Meta to its focus list with a $940 target, plus Wells Fargo $995, Truist $1,000, UBS $815, KeyBanc, Cantor, Guggenheim, BofA, Goldman and Pivotal; median target near $848) cite core ad strength (Q3 revenue +26%), Q4 revenue guidance of $73B-$76B, above consensus, and early Muse AI traction (Citi sees $27B of Muse revenue by 2030); Investing.com notes a modest ~21x trailing P/E, falling to about 17.6x 2027 earnings, and repeated large bond sales (including $15B on Nov 16) plus Moody's stable Aa3 rating show strong credit demand.
- **Bear:** Analysts (Needham, Scotiabank at Hold with $650) cite dilution from a ~$97.5B equity plan and unproven Muse profitability; another $15B bond sale on Nov 16 on top of about $25B earlier this month, negative Q3 free cash flow, capex of $135B-$145B in 2026 (Wells Fargo sees ~$250B in 2027) and S&P's negative outlook point to rising leverage, which Investing.com linked to AI-debt worries; regulators keep pressing (an €805M Italian privacy fine, a £450,000 Ofcom fine, EU orders on WhatsApp AI access, Canada's Bill C-22), a Delaware suit has frozen the Zuckerberg share plan, and shares fell 3.27% on Nov 19 to about $596, a fourth straight loss.
