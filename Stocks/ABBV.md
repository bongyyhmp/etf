## Bull case / bear case

- **Bull:** Supporters cite the Q3 beat with Skyrizi and Rinvoq each growing 20%+, another 2026 EPS guidance raise, a 5.2% dividend increase and pipeline additions (etentamig filing, Qulipta's TEMPLE data, Nxera IBD licensing, the DISCO bispecific option deal), plus a 10th U.S. Rinvoq indication in vitiligo (Nov 23) and plant and R&D investments; Piper Sandler ($349, raised Nov 18), Morgan Stanley ($346), Wells Fargo ($340) and Barclays ($335) have lifted targets, and BMO expects Skyrizi to hold an efficacy edge over J&J's oral Icotyde near term.
