## Bull case / bear case

- **Bull:** Supporters point to the Q3 beat with Skyrizi and Rinvoq each growing 20%+, another 2026 EPS guidance raise, a 5.2% dividend increase and pipeline additions like the etentamig filing, Qulipta's tolerability edge over topiramate in the TEMPLE head-to-head study, the Nov 4 Nxera IBD licensing deal and an expanded Oxford R&D site; Morgan Stanley ($346) and Wells Fargo ($340) raised targets on Nov 2, Investing.com cites a ~$306 median target, and BMO (Nov 9) expects Skyrizi's efficacy edge over J&J's oral Icotyde to support near-term growth.
- **Bear:** 24/7 Wall St. (Hold, $291.77 target, Nov 3) and others flag a full valuation (about 21x forward earnings) with shares near that target, heavy reliance on Skyrizi and Rinvoq (nearly half of Q3 revenue, per Investing.com; Trefis on Nov 12 noted they drive almost all of 2026's expected revenue growth and flagged ~7.9x sales and ~$70B net debt), a 31% Humira decline and 26% Imbruvica decline, a modest lutikizumab readout, EPS dilution from the Apogee deal, and BMO's view that oral rival Icotyde should gain psoriatic arthritis share over time.
