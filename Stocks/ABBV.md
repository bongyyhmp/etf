([source](https://www.tikr.com/blog/abbvies-pipeline-had-a-mixed-week-the-october-30-report-has-to-show-its-guidance-still-rises))
- **2026-10-22** — Goldman Sachs raised its target to $294 from $258 while keeping a Neutral rating. ([source](https://www.ad-hoc-news.de/boerse/news/corporate-news/goldman-sachs-lifts-target-for-abbvie-stock-to-usd-294-00/70415597))

([source](https://www.biopharminternational.com/view/abbvie-bla-fda-etentamig-relapsed-refractory-multiple-myeloma))
- **2026-10-26** — Piper Sandler reaffirmed its Buy rating with a $303 target ahead of Q3 results. ([source](https://www.ad-hoc-news.de/boerse/news/corporate-news/piper-sandler-reaffirms-buy-on-abbvie-stock-the-price-gains-1-69-percent/70419414))

([source](https://247wallst.com/investing/2026/10/26/abbvie-options-price-in-a-sudden-5-swing-what-investors-must-know-before-oct-30/))
- **2026-10-23** — The FDA approved a subcutaneous induction regimen for Skyrizi in Crohn's disease, which commentators expect to support growth from early 2027. ([source](https://247wallst.com/investing/2026/10/26/abbvie-options-price-in-a-sudden-5-swing-what-investors-must-know-before-oct-30/))
