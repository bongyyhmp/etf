## Bull case / bear case

- **Bull:** Fiscal Q4 beat on revenue ($10.41B, +53%) and EPS ($4.16), and the fiscal Q1 guide ($11.10B, $4.35) came in above consensus, with record free cash flow, the largest quarterly buyback in over eight years and management targeting 40%+ chip-equipment growth in calendar 2026 on AI demand; DRAM rose to about a third of systems sales, and before the print Citi and UBS (Buy, $695) were positive and TipRanks showed a Strong Buy consensus.
- **Bear:** Demand stays cyclical and tied to customer capex; coverage flags China (28%-30% of sales) export-control and tariff risk, component shortages at peers, and Dickerson said clean-room availability will limit what Applied can ship next year; shares fell 3.6% into the print and remain about half their June record, B. Riley and Morgan Stanley cut targets beforehand, and the after-hours reaction to the beat was muted and mixed.
