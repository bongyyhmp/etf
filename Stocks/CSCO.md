## Bull case / bear case

- **Bull:** The Nov 12 report beat on revenue ($18.5B, +24%) and EPS ($1.40), with networking up about 43%; Cisco raised fiscal 2027 revenue and EPS guidance and lifted its hyperscaler AI revenue target to $9B, and analysts had cited AI networking orders (BofA, Buy, $150), a campus refresh, Splunk-driven software and an average target near $135-$137.
- **Bear:** Shares fell 5.4% on Nov 13 as Citi, Wells Fargo, Barclays, Piper Sandler and Rosenblatt cut targets, citing product-order growth slowing to 21% from 35%, possible peak growth and Splunk weakness; non-GAAP gross margin of 65.6% and the 65%-66% Q2 guide sit below last year's ~68% as lower-margin hyperscaler hardware grows, the Q2 revenue guide is below Q1's level, and Morgan Stanley and Bernstein had warned before the print that some orders were pulled ahead of price hikes.
