## Bull case / bear case

- **Bull:** Analysts cite hyperscaler AI networking orders (BofA: ~$6B AI backlog, Buy, $150), a campus-switch refresh cycle, growing Splunk-driven software revenue, Morgan Stanley's view of multi-year double-digit earnings growth (with estimates above guidance) and a Moderate Buy consensus (MarketBeat average target about $128.52) heading into the Nov 12 report.
- **Bear:** Morgan Stanley's downgrade to Equal-Weight ($120) argues the AI rerating is largely priced in at ~19x forward earnings; coverage flags 30%-40% gross margins on hyperscaler AI sales, memory costs, Arista competition, possibly pulled-forward orders, recent insider selling and a sharp Nov 5 drop.
