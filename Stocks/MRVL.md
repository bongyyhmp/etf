## Bull case / bear case

- **Bull:** Analysts (Piper Sandler, UBS, Jefferies, SPDB) cite AI custom-chip and optical demand, investor-day targets, and see Lumentum lasers as strategically valuable against Broadcom.
- **Bear:** Investors worry a reported all-stock Lumentum deal would mean heavy dilution at a rich price, and coverage flags exposure to any hyperscaler spending pullback (including at customer Google), customer concentration and custom-chip margin pressure.
