## Bull case / bear case

- **Bull:** Analysts (Piper Sandler, Jefferies; MarketBeat consensus Moderate Buy) and Morningstar cite AI custom-chip and optical demand and a strong fit with Lumentum's lasers; management promises first-year EPS accretion, over $1B in cost synergies, a $70-90B fiscal-2031 revenue goal and fiscal Q3 revenue tracking guidance, and the stock gained another 6.4% on Nov 11 as CoreWeave's results lifted AI suppliers.
- **Bear:** BofA and TD Cowen downgraded and several firms (including Cantor) cut targets over dilution from the all-stock Lumentum deal, which analysts cited by Bloomberg give roughly coin-flip odds of closing; the Motley Fool flags data-center moratorium risk and a long review including China, GuruFocus calls the stock modestly overvalued, and it is still down about 40% over the past month.
