## Bull case / bear case

- **Bull:** Analysts (Piper Sandler, UBS, Jefferies, SPDB; MarketBeat consensus Moderate Buy) and Morningstar cite AI custom-chip and optical demand and a strong fit with Lumentum's lasers; management promises first-year EPS accretion, over $1B in cost synergies and a $70-90B fiscal-2031 revenue goal.
- **Bear:** The all-stock Lumentum deal gives Lumentum holders about a third of the company; BofA and TD Cowen downgraded on Nov 3, and investors and Morningstar worry about dilution, overpaying and a long road to a 2028 close; coverage also flags hyperscaler-spending risk (including customer Google) and customer concentration.
