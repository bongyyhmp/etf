## Bull case / bear case

- **Bull:** Analysts (Piper Sandler, Jefferies; MarketBeat consensus Moderate Buy) and Morningstar cite AI custom-chip and optical demand and a strong fit with Lumentum's lasers; management promises first-year EPS accretion, over $1B in cost synergies, a $70-90B fiscal-2031 revenue goal and fiscal Q3 revenue tracking guidance.
- **Bear:** BofA and TD Cowen downgraded and several firms cut targets over dilution from the all-stock Lumentum deal; the Motley Fool flags data-center moratorium risk, a deal value that shrinks with Marvell's price, and a long regulatory review including China, after the stock fell roughly half from its high.
