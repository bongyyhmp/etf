## Bull case / bear case

- **Bull:** Q3 delivered about 19% revenue growth, record auto revenue and a $5B buyback, and the quarterly dividend stays at $1.014; StoneX initiated at Buy ($340), Cantor (Overweight, $400) and Truist (Buy, $300) remain positive, MarketBeat's consensus is Moderate Buy (average target about $296), software-defined-vehicle revenue is tracking toward a $2B 2027 goal and 2026 data-center sales are expected to top $500M, and its less AI-heavy mix meant a smaller drop (about 4%) than the chip index on Nov 20.
- **Bear:** Q4 revenue guidance (about $3.85B midpoint) landed slightly under consensus, Stifel, Morgan Stanley and Truist trimmed targets, a shrinking mobile business was flagged, and peer Microchip's weak outlook revived questions about industrial and auto demand; coverage called the robotics launch strategic rather than a near-term revenue driver, insiders sold small stakes in November, the new EUR 1B bond adds debt, and shares kept falling with the chip sector, including about 4.3% on Nov 20 amid AI-spending doubts and new export rules.
