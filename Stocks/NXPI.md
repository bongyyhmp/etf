## Bull case / bear case

- **Bull:** Q3 delivered about 19% revenue growth, record auto revenue and a $5B buyback, and the quarterly dividend stays at $1.014; StoneX initiated at Buy ($340), citing leadership in automotive radar and less dependence on AI data-center spending, Cantor (Overweight, $400) and Truist (Buy, $300) remain positive, MarketBeat's consensus is Moderate Buy (average target about $296), software-defined-vehicle revenue is tracking toward a $2B 2027 goal, and Finexus cited channel checks pointing to the end of auto-chip destocking as shares rose about 4% on Nov. 27.
- **Bear:** Q4 revenue guidance (about $3.85B midpoint) landed slightly under consensus, Stifel, Morgan Stanley and Truist trimmed targets, a shrinking mobile business was flagged and peer Microchip's weak outlook revived industrial and auto demand questions; the new EUR 1B bond adds debt, insiders sold small stakes in November, analog peers ADI (-17%) and TXN slumped on Nov. 24 amid a prolonged industrial and auto trough (TradingKey), and euro-quoted shares slid to about EUR 210 on Nov. 25 amid tariff worries before partly recovering to about EUR 223 on Nov. 26 and about $253 in U.S. trading on Nov. 27.
