## Bull case / bear case

- **Bull:** Bulls (Yorkville's Dan Ives, CFRA, TD Cowen and Guggenheim at Buy; TradingKey cites a ~$402 average target) cite Google Cloud revenue more than doubling, a growing external TPU business (Bloomberg Intelligence sees TPU sales above $135B by 2028), a ~$121B minimum Anthropic commitment and an Anthropic stake valued at roughly $132B-$139B across the IPO range (Motley Fool, Reuters), ahead of the expected Nov 19 debut; 24/7 Wall St. said Nvidia's CEO exit put TPUs in the spotlight, and recent debt deals (a ~US$3.8B Asian bond drew more than twice that in orders) show strong funding access.
- **Bear:** Bears cite the large Q3 EPS miss, ad revenue below forecasts, signs AI may erode search (Pivotal), antitrust remedy risk, negative free cash flow on rising capex (about -$9.1B in Q3 on $51.4B capex, per 24/7 Wall St.) funded partly with debt and share sales, plus new commitments such as $15B in Malaysia, $10B more into Anthropic and ~$3B of prepaid energy deals; target cuts (JPMorgan, Scotiabank, TD Cowen), weak technicals, a pending securities class action over Gemini 3.5 Pro disclosures, Anthropic's $104B nine-month loss, and a ~3.5% drop on Nov 16 as AI and chip stocks sold off.
