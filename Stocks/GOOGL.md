## Bull case / bear case

- **Bull:** Bulls (Yorkville's Dan Ives, CFRA, TD Cowen and Guggenheim at Buy; consensus target about $400, per 24/7 Wall St.) cite Google Cloud revenue more than doubling, a growing external TPU business (Bloomberg Intelligence sees TPU sales above $135B by 2028), a ~$121B minimum Anthropic commitment and a roughly 13.7% Anthropic stake (Motley Fool) whose value rises with the IPO pricing at $83, above the $70-$78 range (Reuters), ahead of the Nov 19 debut; the Gemini app has reached a billion users, Nvidia's CEO exit put TPUs in the spotlight, and recent debt deals (a ~US$3.8B Asian bond drew more than twice that in orders) show strong funding access.
- **Bear:** Bears cite the large Q3 EPS miss, ad revenue below forecasts, signs AI may erode search (Pivotal), antitrust remedy risk, and negative free cash flow on rising capex (about -$9.1B in Q3 on $51.4B capex) funded partly with debt and share sales, plus new commitments such as $15B in Malaysia, $10B more into Anthropic and ~$3B of prepaid energy deals; 2026 EPS estimates have been cut 45 times in a month and shares sit near a 52-week low below key moving averages (24/7 Wall St.), target cuts came from JPMorgan, Scotiabank and TD Cowen, Druckenmiller's fund exited its stake, and a securities class action over Gemini 3.5 Pro disclosures is pending.
