## Bull case / bear case

- **Bull:** Bulls (Yorkville's Dan Ives, CFRA, TD Cowen and Guggenheim at Buy; consensus target about $400 per 24/7 Wall St.) cite Google Cloud revenue more than doubling, a growing external TPU business (Bloomberg Intelligence sees $135B+ of TPU sales by 2028), a ~$121B Anthropic commitment and a ~13.4%-13.7% Anthropic stake, a billion-user Gemini app and a modest ~18.8x 2027 earnings multiple (Trefis); an oversubscribed Asian bond and a new C$6B note sale show ready access to funding, and Kuo ties the reported TPU 8i trim to supply and power limits rather than demand.
- **Bear:** Bears cite the Q3 EPS miss, ad revenue below forecasts, AI risk to search, antitrust remedies and capex that consumed 89% of operating cash flow (Trefis), with Q3 free cash flow negative and growing reliance on debt; 2026 EPS estimates were cut 45 times in a month (24/7 Wall St.), JPMorgan, Scotiabank and TD Cowen trimmed targets, Druckenmiller's fund exited, shares fell to about $265 on Nov 20, below the prior 52-week low and roughly a third under the May high, Kuo reports 2027 TPU shipment cuts, and a securities class action over Gemini 3.5 Pro disclosures is pending.
