## Bull case / bear case

- **Bull:** Bulls (Yorkville's Dan Ives, CFRA, TD Cowen and Guggenheim at Buy) point to Google Cloud revenue more than doubling with rising margins, an external TPU business (Oracle as first buyer) and a selloff they see as overdone.
- **Bear:** Bears cite a large EPS miss, ad revenue below forecasts, signs AI may be eroding search (Pivotal), negative free cash flow on capex of up to $205B for 2026 now partly debt-funded, and a wave of target cuts (Scotiabank, Raymond James, Susquehanna, KeyBanc, Wells Fargo).
