## Bull case / bear case

- **Bull:** Bulls (Yorkville's Dan Ives, CFRA, TD Cowen and Guggenheim at Buy; Benzinga cites a ~$398 average target) cite Google Cloud revenue more than doubling, a growing external TPU business (Bloomberg Intelligence sees TPU sales above $135B by 2028), a ~$121B minimum Anthropic commitment and a stake in Anthropic the Motley Fool values near $132B at the IPO midpoint.
- **Bear:** Bears cite the large Q3 EPS miss, ad revenue below forecasts, signs AI may erode search (Pivotal), negative free cash flow on rising capex (plus new commitments such as $15B in Malaysia and $10B more into Anthropic), possible data-center curbs from a Democratic Congress, target cuts (JPMorgan, Scotiabank, TD Cowen and others) and weak technicals, with shares below their 20- and 200-day averages (Benzinga).
