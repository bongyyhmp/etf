## Bull case / bear case

- **Bull:** Bulls (Yorkville's Dan Ives, CFRA, TD Cowen and Guggenheim at Buy) point to Google Cloud revenue more than doubling with rising margins, an external TPU business, a disclosed ~$121B minimum Anthropic spending commitment, a selloff they see as overdone, and (per Nov 4 coverage) hopes that slower AI capex could restore free cash flow.
- **Bear:** Bears cite a large EPS miss, ad revenue below forecasts, signs AI may be eroding search (Pivotal), negative free cash flow on capex of up to $205B for 2026 now partly debt-funded, possible new curbs on data centers from a Democratic Congress, and a wave of target cuts (Scotiabank, Raymond James, Susquehanna, KeyBanc, Wells Fargo).
