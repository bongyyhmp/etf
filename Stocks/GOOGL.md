## Bull case / bear case

- **Bull:** Bulls (Yorkville's Dan Ives, CFRA, TD Cowen and Guggenheim at Buy; consensus target about $400, per 24/7 Wall St.) cite Google Cloud revenue more than doubling, a growing external TPU business (Bloomberg Intelligence sees TPU sales above $135B by 2028), a ~$121B minimum Anthropic commitment and a roughly 13.4%-13.7% Anthropic stake that GuruFocus valued near $147B at the $83 IPO price, with ANT closing its first day 17% higher at $97.17; the Gemini app has reached a billion users, Trefis puts the stock near 18.8x 2027 earnings, and recent debt deals (a ~US$3.8B Asian bond drew more than twice that in orders) show strong funding access.
- **Bear:** Bears cite the large Q3 EPS miss, ad revenue below forecasts, signs AI may erode search (Pivotal), antitrust remedy risk, and capex that ate 89% of operating cash flow over the past year (Trefis) with Q3 free cash flow negative, funded partly with debt and share sales, plus new commitments such as $15B in Malaysia, $10B more into Anthropic and ~$3B of prepaid energy deals; 2026 EPS estimates have been cut 45 times in a month (24/7 Wall St.), the Anthropic debut gave no lift as shares fell about 3.5% on Nov 19 to around their 52-week low, roughly 30% below the May high, target cuts came from JPMorgan, Scotiabank and TD Cowen, Druckenmiller's fund exited, and a securities class action over Gemini 3.5 Pro disclosures is pending.
