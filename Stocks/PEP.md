## Bull case / bear case

- **Bull:** Trefis cites a Q3 gross margin of 54.4%, up from a year earlier, 3.1% organic revenue growth with full-year organic guidance held at 3.0%, management's expectation of faster sales growth in Q4 and 2027, and about $8.9B returned to shareholders in fiscal 2026; Mitrade notes a dividend yield of about 4.2%.
- **Bear:** Management cut its 2026 core EPS growth outlook in October (to about 3% at the midpoint per Trefis), Q3 core operating margin slipped to 16.9% from 17.3%, North American beverage volume fell 2%, and an Elliott-backed margin-improvement promise has not yet been delivered (Mitrade); Trefis flags rising commodity costs as hedges roll off and an uncertain North America beverage recovery.
