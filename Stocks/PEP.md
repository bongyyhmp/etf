## Bull case / bear case

- **Bull:** Trefis cites a Q3 gross margin of 54.4%, up from a year earlier, 3.1% organic revenue growth with full-year organic guidance held at 3.0%, management's expectation of faster sales growth in Q4 and 2027, and about $8.9B returned to shareholders in fiscal 2026; the Motley Fool and Yahoo Finance highlight a ~4% yield, 54 straight years of dividend increases (latest quarterly payout $1.48) and a valuation under 17x earnings, about half Coca-Cola's, with a Fool projection favoring PepsiCo over 10 years if its multiple recovers; Bernstein sees international operations partly offsetting North American weakness.
- **Bear:** Management cut its 2026 core EPS growth outlook to 2.5%-3.5% from 5%-7%, Q3 core operating margin slipped to 16.9% from 17.3%, North American beverage volume fell 2% and North America foods core operating profit dropped 12%; Trefis flags rising commodity costs as hedges roll off, Bernstein downgraded to Underperform ($128) on Nov 17 expecting earnings shortfalls, Wolfe (Peer Perform) says Coca-Cola keeps taking share, the Motley Fool notes higher leverage and heavy reliance on Walmart (~14% of revenue), and an Elliott-backed margin plan has yet to deliver (Mitrade).
