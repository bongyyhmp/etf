## Bull case / bear case

- **Bull:** Q3 beat with a raised 2026 EPS outlook, Keytruda Qlex ahead of estimates, Winrevair up 93% with nearly 1,600 new U.S. patients, positive Phase 2b MASH data for efinopegdutide on Nov 9, and brokers (JPMorgan $175, BofA, Guggenheim, Barclays) citing pipeline assets such as sac-TMT, intismeran and Lipfendra.
