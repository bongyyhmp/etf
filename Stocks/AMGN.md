- **Bull:** Bulls cite the Q3 beat and raised 2026 guidance, fast growth at Repatha (+37%, with new AHA cost-effectiveness data), Evenity, Imdelltra and Uplizna, progressing MariTide Phase 3 studies and positive Phase 3 dazodalibep data in Sjögren's, a settlement clearing an Oct 2028 U.S. launch of its Opdivo biosimilar, with shares up 5.5% on Nov 24 near their 52-week high; Piper Sandler ($542, raised from $457), Goldman ($536), TD Cowen ($531), UBS ($530) and Argus ($515, Buy) are among the more positive.
- **Bear:** Bears note dazodalibep's dryness benefit was significant at only one dose with Novartis's ianalumab a rival (Fierce Biotech), biosimilars nearly halved Prolia and XGEVA sales and Trefis warns the next biosimilars may launch late; commentators also flag IRA price pressure, about $57B of debt and a near-record valuation (Trefis: ~26.8x trailing earnings), average targets (~$415 MarketBeat, ~$422 TradingKey, where most covering analysts rate it Hold) well below the ~$474 price, Freedom Broker's downgrade to Hold, and neutral views from Barclays ($430), Mizuho ($388), Morgan Stanley ($410) and Bernstein ($400).
