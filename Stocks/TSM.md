## Bull case / bear case

- **Bull:** Analysts (Bernstein Outperform at $600, Barclays, Needham) and Moody's cite manufacturing leadership and AI demand; October revenue rose about 60% year over year to a record, Q3 EPS beat for a third straight quarter, Q4 guidance implies about 14% sequential growth, an executive bought shares on Nov. 20, and the shares bounced with the chip group (ADR +14% on Nov. 23 and +3.2% on Nov. 27; Taipei limit-up on Nov. 24).
- **Bear:** Coverage flags heavy capex, overseas fabs and N2 costs weighing on margins, Taiwan concentration and geopolitical risk, USTR's proposed Section 301 tariff of up to 15% on Taiwan goods, whose report targets Taiwan's chip sector (reportedly not stacking on chip tariffs; comments due Jan. 5), a new U.S. rule that has paused some shipments to Chinese customers pending licenses, and heavy reliance on Nvidia (about 22% of 2026 revenue per TradingKey) amid its regulatory scrutiny; foreign investors kept net selling Taiwan stocks (about NT$123.7B on Nov. 25, NT$49.2B on Nov. 26 and NT$29.6B on Nov. 27), GuruFocus calls the stock fairly valued, and the ADR remains well below its October record.
