## Bull case / bear case

- **Bull:** Analysts (Barclays, Needham) and Moody's cite manufacturing leadership and AI demand; October revenue hit a record, up 59.7% year over year and 14.7% from September, Q4 guidance implies about 14% sequential growth, TradingKey reports Buy ratings with an average target near $574, and Taiwan curbed short selling and readied a stabilization fund.
- **Bear:** Coverage flags heavy capex (board approved another ~$40.9B of appropriations), Taiwan concentration and geopolitical risk that Anthropic's own filing cites, heavy foreign selling of Taiwan stocks, dependence on a few AI customers and sensitivity to AI-spending scares; GuruFocus sees the ADR near fair value and TradingKey notes memory-entry reports could muddy its pure-play logic model.
