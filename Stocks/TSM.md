## Bull case / bear case

- **Bull:** Analysts (Bernstein Outperform at $600, Barclays, Needham) and Moody's cite manufacturing leadership and AI demand; October revenue rose about 60% year over year to a record, Q3 EPS beat for a third straight quarter, Q4 guidance implies about 14% sequential growth, an executive bought shares on Nov. 20, and Huang's return sent the ADR up 14% on Nov. 23 and the Taipei shares limit-up on Nov. 24.
- **Bear:** Coverage flags heavy capex, overseas fabs and N2 costs weighing on margins, Taiwan concentration and geopolitical risk, a planned U.S. excess-capacity tariff naming Taiwan (TIER expects the combined rate to stay at or below 15%), a new U.S. rule that has paused some shipments to Chinese customers pending licenses, and heavy reliance on Nvidia (about 22% of 2026 revenue per TradingKey) amid its SEC scrutiny; foreign investors sold about NT$123.7B of Taiwan stocks on Nov. 25, GuruFocus calls the stock fairly valued, and the ADR remains well below its October record.
