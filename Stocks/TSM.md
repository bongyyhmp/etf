## Bull case / bear case

- **Bull:** Analysts (Barclays, Needham) and Moody's cite manufacturing leadership and AI demand; Q4 guidance implies about 14% sequential growth, TradingKey reports Buy ratings with an average target near $574, and the shares rebounded on Nov 6-9 as Taiwan curbed short selling and readied a stabilization fund.
- **Bear:** Coverage flags heavy capex ($60-64B guided for 2026), Taiwan concentration, heavy foreign selling of Taiwan stocks, dependence on a few AI customers and sharp sensitivity to AI-spending and US political scares; TradingKey notes the Nvidia-linked memory reports could pressure its pure-play logic model, and GuruFocus sees the ADR near fair value.
