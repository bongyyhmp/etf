## Bull case / bear case

- **Bull:** Analysts (Barclays, Needham) and Moody's cite manufacturing leadership and AI demand; October revenue hit a record, up 59.7% year over year, Q4 guidance implies about 14% sequential growth, top customer Nvidia beat Q3 forecasts and guided higher, TradingKey reports Buy ratings with an average target near $574, and GuruFocus (GF Score 99/100) sees the new U.S. rule costing screening time rather than leading-edge demand.
- **Bear:** Coverage flags heavy capex (another ~$40.9B of appropriations approved), overseas fabs and N2 costs diluting margins, Taiwan concentration and geopolitical risk, a new U.S. rule adding foundry licensing checks, heavy dependence on Nvidia (about 22% of 2026 revenue per TradingKey) amid its CEO turmoil, and persistent foreign selling in Taipei, where the stock fell another 2% on Nov 17 after a 5.7% drop on Nov 16.
