## Bull case / bear case

- **Bull:** Analysts (Barclays, Needham) and Moody's cite manufacturing leadership and AI demand; October revenue hit a record NT$586.9B, up 59.7% year over year, Q4 guidance implies about 14% sequential growth and top customer Nvidia beat and raised. TradingKey reports Buy ratings with an average target near $574, GuruFocus sees the new U.S. foundry rule adding screening time rather than costing leading-edge demand and says the selloff isn't tied to weaker orders, and an industry insider expects China phone-chip licenses to be granted.
- **Bear:** Coverage flags heavy capex, overseas fabs and N2 costs weighing on margins, Taiwan concentration and geopolitical risk, a new U.S. rule that has paused some shipments to Chinese customers pending licenses, and heavy reliance on Nvidia (about 22% of 2026 revenue per TradingKey) amid its CEO turmoil, SEC scrutiny and possible supplier cost pressure. Foreign investors keep selling in Taipei, where TSMC fell 4.76% on Nov. 20 as Taiwan had its worst week since 1991, and the ADR slid about 8.5% intraday.
