## Bull case / bear case

- **Bull:** Analysts (Bernstein Outperform at $600, Barclays, Needham) and Moody's cite manufacturing leadership and AI demand; October revenue rose about 60% year over year to a record, Q3 EPS beat for a third straight quarter, Q4 guidance implies about 14% sequential growth, an executive bought shares on Nov. 20, retail investors bought heavily in Taipei, and the ADR jumped 14% on Nov. 23 as Huang's return eased worries about top customer Nvidia.
- **Bear:** Coverage flags heavy capex, overseas fabs and N2 costs weighing on margins, Taiwan concentration and geopolitical risk, a new U.S. rule that has paused some shipments to Chinese customers pending licenses, and heavy reliance on Nvidia (about 22% of 2026 revenue per TradingKey) amid its SEC scrutiny and ongoing investigation; foreign investors sold heavily in Taipei last week, where the stock fell to a seven-month low on Nov. 20 and remains far below its October high.
