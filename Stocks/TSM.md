## Bull case / bear case

- **Bull:** Analysts (Barclays, Needham) and Moody's cite manufacturing leadership and AI demand; October revenue hit a record, up 59.7% year over year, Q4 guidance implies about 14% sequential growth, the chairman has said demand far exceeds supply, TradingKey reports Buy ratings with an average target near $574, and GuruFocus gives it a 98/100 GF Score.
- **Bear:** Coverage flags heavy capex (another ~$40.9B of appropriations approved), overseas fabs diluting gross margins, Taiwan concentration and geopolitical risk that Anthropic's own filing cites, dependence on a few AI customers such as Nvidia, whose CEO exit weighed on the ADR on Nov 12 and 13, and sensitivity to AI-spending scares; GuruFocus sees the ADR near fair value.
