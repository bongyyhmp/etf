## Bull case / bear case

- **Bull:** Analysts (Morgan Stanley's Joseph Moore, Wedbush, RBC, Fubon, Rosenblatt) cite AI-driven memory demand, FQ1 revenue guidance of about $61.5B with ~86% gross margin and more than 75% of fiscal 2027 output committed; Morgan Stanley says lower valuations improve the risk-reward, 24/7 Wall St. counts 45 of 49 analysts at Buy, GuruFocus calls it fairly valued near 10.6x earnings, and buybacks may accelerate after Dec 9.
- **Bear:** 24/7 Wall St. and a Motley Fool contributor flag cycle risk, citing the CFO's comment that price gains will moderate, capped pricing in long-term deals and new capacity from Micron, Samsung, SK Hynix and China's CXMT; coverage also notes exposure to Nvidia, whose CEO exit hit the stock on Nov 12 and 13 (the Motley Fool notes Nvidia is Micron's biggest customer and backstops neoclouds whose stumbles could sour sentiment), a paused $4.5B Taiwan plant, insider selling, post-midterm data-center moratoria and a price about 35% below its June record.
