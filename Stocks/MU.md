## Bull case / bear case

- **Bull:** Analysts (Wedbush, RBC, Fubon, Rosenblatt) cite AI-driven memory demand, FQ1 revenue guidance of about $61.5B, multi-year pricing deals and the $35B buyback that could accelerate after Dec 9.
- **Bear:** Coverage flags peak-cycle worries and price pressure from added capacity (including China's CXMT), with the stock about a third below its high after a three-session drop tied to Samsung's miss and Kioxia's cautious outlook.
