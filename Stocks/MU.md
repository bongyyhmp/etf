## Bull case / bear case

- **Bull:** Analysts (Wedbush, RBC, Fubon, Rosenblatt) cite AI-driven memory demand, FQ1 revenue guidance of about $61.5B, more than 75% of fiscal 2027 output committed and a buyback that could accelerate after Dec 9; 24/7 Wall St. counts 45 of 49 analysts at Buy, and TIKR notes a forward P/E under 5x after the selloff.
- **Bear:** Coverage flags peak-cycle worries (TIKR notes the low multiple resembles the 2017-18 peak), added capacity including China's CXMT, cautious peer outlooks, a reported pause of a $4.5B Taiwan plant and post-midterm data-center moratoria that UBS says could hit about 30% of 2028 U.S. additions; the stock remains far below its $1,255 52-week high.
