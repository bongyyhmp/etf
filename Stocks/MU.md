## Bull case / bear case

- **Bull:** Analysts (Morgan Stanley, Wedbush, RBC Buy at $1,500, KeyBanc Overweight at $1,750, Bernstein Buy at $1,300) cite AI-driven memory demand, FQ1 guidance of about $61.5B revenue with ~86% gross margin and roughly 75% of 2027 output committed; the CEO says supply will be even tighter in 2027-28, backed by $32B of take-or-pay commitments. A Motley Fool contributor notes about 4.4x forward earnings, Barron's called memory stocks very cheap, and buybacks may pick up after CHIPS Act limits lapse Dec. 9.
- **Bear:** 24/7 Wall St. and Motley Fool contributors flag cycle risk: gross margins near 87% leave little room to expand, 2028 pricing could soften, long-term deals cap prices, and Micron, Samsung, SK Hynix and CXMT are adding capacity. Coverage also notes heavy reliance on Nvidia for HBM amid its leadership and SEC turmoil, SemiAnalysis's lower AI capex forecasts, a DRAM price-fixing class action and new U.S. export rules; the stock lost about 22% in five sessions through a ~13% drop on Nov. 20.
