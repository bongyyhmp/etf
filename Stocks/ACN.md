## Bull case / bear case

- **Bull:** Bulls cite a valuation below its own average (about 17.5x earnings per Simply Wall St, 18.3x per Trefis), record Q4 bookings of $22.2B and $84.5B for fiscal 2026 (book-to-bill 1.1), fiscal 2026 operating margin rising to 15.8%, more than 400 clients starting advanced AI projects, new AI-led outsourcing deals (Qantas, Mondelez), federal defense work, a new Data-as-a-Service line, bolt-on deals such as the just-closed Industries eXcellence purchase, ~$11.7B of free cash flow, a $3M CEO share purchase and $5.5B of planned FY27 buybacks.
- **Bear:** Bears flag a 3.6% drop on Nov 18 that TradingKey tied to cautious client budgets, longer sales cycles and pricing pressure (average target near $238, below recent prices), lower pricing in many areas in Q4, roughly 3% organic growth, acquisition-dependent revenue and debt up to $13.4B, a 3%-6% FY27 outlook, a weak Middle East business and a stock still about 32% below its 2025 peak (Trefis), the risk AI shrinks client spend (Trefis, Baird), possible compliance scrutiny after a reported AI-agent incident on a DoD network, and reputational questions after outside checks found citation errors in its research (Accenture blames human error and is correcting the paper).
