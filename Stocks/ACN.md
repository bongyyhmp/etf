## Bull case / bear case

- **Bull:** Bulls cite a valuation below its own average and the S&P 500 median (Trefis), record Q4 bookings of $22.2B (Zacks), new federal defense work and agentic-AI client projects such as the Mondelez order-to-cash overhaul, a $3M CEO share purchase, and its own CIO survey showing large unmet demand for help scaling AI.
- **Bear:** Bears flag lower pricing in many areas, roughly 3% organic growth, acquisition-dependent revenue and higher debt, a 3%-6% FY27 outlook, the risk AI shrinks client spend (Trefis, Baird), and possible compliance scrutiny after a reported AI-agent incident on a DoD network.
