## Bull case / bear case

- **Bull:** Bulls cite a valuation below its own average and the S&P 500 median (Trefis), record Q4 bookings of $22.2B (Zacks), new federal defense work, agentic-AI client projects such as the Mondelez order-to-cash overhaul, a new Data-as-a-Service line for AI training and evaluation, a $3M CEO share purchase, $5.5B of planned FY27 buybacks (Trefis), and its own CIO survey showing large unmet demand for help scaling AI.
- **Bear:** Bears flag lower pricing in many areas, roughly 3% organic growth, acquisition-dependent revenue and higher debt, a 3%-6% FY27 outlook, the risk AI shrinks client spend (Trefis, Baird), possible compliance scrutiny after a reported AI-agent incident on a DoD network, and reputational questions after outside checks found citation errors in its research publications (Accenture blames human error and is correcting the paper).
