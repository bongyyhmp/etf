## Bull case / bear case

- **Bull:** Q3 beat estimates for a seventh straight quarter, with $16B of operating cash flow, falling net debt and record refinery throughput of about 1.09M bpd (Valor); Morgan Stanley sees a higher FCF outlook, 24/7 Wall St. cites a ~$242 consensus target, Trefis notes a lower valuation than Exxon (about 15.8x vs. 17.0x trailing earnings) with a higher operating margin, and commentators point to Gulf of Mexico growth (the FT reports a near-final ~$5B Beacon Offshore deal), exploration (including QatarEnergy's farm-in to its Namibia licence), Venezuela, Vaca Muerta and West Qurna-2 in Iraq, plus an integrated model in which refining gains offset lower output (Motley Fool); Barclays edged its target up to $248 on Nov 13 and Berkshire held its ~$18.6B stake steady in Q3.
- **Bear:** Commentators flag dependence on oil and refining margins tied to Middle East tensions (shares fell about 9.7% in the week to Nov 6; Brent slid toward $90 by Nov 10, swung back toward $96 on Nov 12, then fell about 4% to near $92 on Nov 16 as Hormuz flows recovered and below $90 on Nov 18 after a big U.S. inventory build, when Chevron dropped about 4.7%; Bernstein, which cut its target to $233 on Nov 12 at Market Perform, sees Iran de-escalation as more likely), Q3 output down year over year (Rigzone), 2027 EPS consensus below 2026, buybacks cut to $2.5B, easing refining margins and investor unease over spending on the reported Beacon purchase rather than buybacks (TradingKey), DZ Bank's Nov 3 downgrade to Hold, Jefferies' warning on data-center risks to Project Kilby, and political pressure over 'windfall' profits.
