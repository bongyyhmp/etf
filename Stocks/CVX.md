## Bull case / bear case

- **Bull:** Bulls cite a seventh straight quarterly beat, $16B of Q3 operating cash flow, falling net debt and record refinery throughput (Valor); Morgan Stanley sees a higher FCF outlook, 24/7 Wall St. cites a ~$242 consensus target, Trefis notes a lower valuation than Exxon with a higher operating margin, and commentators point to Gulf of Mexico growth (the FT-reported ~$5B Beacon deal), new exploration in Namibia and Greece, Venezuela, Iraq and an integrated model that cushions lower output (Motley Fool); Barclays raised its target to $248 and Berkshire held its stake in Q3.
- **Bear:** Commentators flag dependence on volatile, conflict-driven crude (Brent fell to roughly three-month lows on surplus expectations before a Nov 24 rebound, and Trefis puts Chevron's full-payout breakeven near $80 WTI); Bernstein ($233, Market Perform) sees Iran de-escalation as more likely and Trefis finds Chevron has tended to lag after oil spikes, while others cite lower Q3 output (Rigzone), 2027 EPS consensus below 2026, buybacks cut to $2.5B, easing refining margins (24/7 Wall St., TradingKey), unease over spending on Beacon instead of buybacks, DZ Bank's downgrade to Hold and political 'windfall' pressure.
