## Bull case / bear case

- **Bull:** Q3 beat estimates for a seventh straight quarter, with $16B of operating cash flow, falling net debt and record refinery throughput of about 1.09M bpd (Valor); Morgan Stanley sees a higher FCF outlook, 24/7 Wall St. cites a ~$242 consensus target, and commentators point to exploration (including QatarEnergy's farm-in to its Namibia licence), Venezuela (new PDVSA agreements reportedly due Nov 16), Vaca Muerta and the West Qurna-2 terms in Iraq, plus an integrated model in which refining gains offset lower output (Motley Fool); Freedom Broker lifted its rating to Hold ($218) on Nov 6.
- **Bear:** Commentators flag dependence on oil and refining margins tied to Middle East tensions (shares fell about 9.7% in the week to Nov 6 and Brent slid toward $90 by Nov 10 before swinging back toward $96 on Nov 12 on a Trump post, amid rising Saudi output and U.S. crude stocks; Bernstein sees Iran de-escalation as more likely), Q3 output down year over year (Rigzone), 2027 EPS consensus below 2026, buybacks cut to $2.5B, DZ Bank's Nov 3 downgrade to Hold, Jefferies' warning on data-center risks to Project Kilby, and political pressure over 'windfall' profits.
