## Bull case / bear case

- **Bull:** Some analysts still rate the stock Buy despite cutting targets (Stifel at $205 says fundamentals are intact, Citi at $225 notes about 11x earnings near a prior trough, DA Davidson at $200), Goldman's James Schneider earlier called it a top pick, and the board says operations, customer commitments and the product roadmap continue under interim CEO Colette Kress; the Nov 17 report (guide ~$108B) is the next test of AI demand.
- **Bear:** Huang's abrupt Nov 12 exit drew downgrades to Hold from Bernstein, Truist and Craig-Hallum, an Oppenheimer downgrade with its target pulled (plus Baird's cut a day earlier), with analysts citing a hard-to-fill leadership gap, possible brain drain and cultural damage; coverage also flags reports of China-bound shipments via a network linked to Huang's associate, a shareholder suit, FBI and congressional scrutiny and a roughly 30% one-month decline.
