## Bull case / bear case

- **Bull:** Supporters point to a Q3 beat (revenue $115.8B vs. ~$108.9B expected, data center +110%), a ~$130B Q4 guide above consensus and a raised 75% fiscal 2028 growth outlook that assumes no China data-center sales; management calls the 71-72% gross margin a trough and says Vera Rubin started strongly. Even after cuts, post-earnings targets from Jefferies, Morgan Stanley, Wells Fargo and UBS ($220-260) sit far above the share price, Aptus Capital calls the franchise intact, and LSEG data showed a forward P/E under 10 before Friday's drop.
- **Bear:** Critics cite governance and legal overhangs: the board has ruled out Jensen Huang's return with no permanent CEO named, the 10-Q is delayed by an internal investigation, Reuters reports SEC enforcement staff are examining possible accounting issues, and the DOJ is looking at the Megaspeed link. Freedom Capital and others flag policy risk, large receivables and customer-financing guarantees, at least 11 firms have moved to Hold, margins are guided lower, and the stock fell 14% on Nov. 20 to $125.53, about 48% below its October record.
