## Bull case / bear case

- **Bull:** Q3 beat handily (revenue $115.8B vs. ~$108.9B expected, data center up 110%) and the ~$130B Q4 guide topped consensus, while interim CEO Kress says the roadmap is intact and Vera Rubin is off to a strong start; several analysts kept Buy ratings after cutting targets (Stifel $205, Citi $225, DA Davidson $200), UBS's Tim Arcuri calls the market's implied assumptions draconian, and Huang has said he would return if the board wanted him.
- **Bear:** At least 10 analysts have cut to Hold since Huang's exit (including Bernstein, Truist and HSBC at $170) over leadership, culture and China risks; the WSJ reports an SEC probe into Nvidia's disclosures, the Justice Department is investigating the Megaspeed link, shareholders have sued, Treasury Secretary Bessent wants an outside CEO, gross margin is guided down to 71-72%, and new Commerce rules restrict overseas compute access for Chinese firms; the stock is down roughly a quarter in November.
