## Bull case / bear case

- **Bull:** Some analysts still rate the stock Buy despite cutting targets (Stifel at $205 says fundamentals are intact, Citi at $225 notes about 11x earnings near a prior trough, DA Davidson at $200), and the board says operations, customer commitments and the roadmap continue under interim CEO Colette Kress, who has pledged to honor ~$164.5B of existing data-center commitments and says supply should stay a bottleneck through fiscal 2028; supporters including large shareholders and a 180,000-signature petition back Huang's return, and the Nov 17 report (consensus ~$108.7B) is the next test of AI demand.
- **Bear:** Huang's abrupt exit drew downgrades to Hold from Bernstein, Truist, Craig-Hallum, HSBC ($170 target) and Baird, plus an Oppenheimer downgrade, with analysts citing a leadership gap, brain drain and cultural damage; Treasury Secretary Bessent wants an outside CEO, the board is running a Wachtell Lipton probe, a new Commerce rule tightens overseas compute access for Chinese firms, and coverage flags Megaspeed-linked China shipments, FBI and congressional scrutiny, a likely more cautious stance on financing deals and a stock that fell another 4.1% on Nov 16 to about 35% below its October high.
