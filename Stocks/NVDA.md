## Bull case / bear case

- **Bull:** Supporters point to Huang's unanimous reinstatement (with the board citing U.S. government assurances on security), after which about a dozen analysts upgraded back to buy or reiterated buy ratings per StreetInsider (Bernstein to Outperform at $200), plus a Q3 beat (revenue $115.8B), a ~$130B Q4 guide above consensus, a raised 75% fiscal 2028 growth outlook and management's view that the 71-72% gross margin is a trough; post-earnings targets ($200-260) sit well above the share price, the Motley Fool notes a trailing P/E near 13.5x, and the NYT says the SEC inquiry is early-stage and may end without charges; Moody's kept its Aa1/stable rating, and the legacy 2018 crypto suit was settled for $290M, mostly insurer-funded.
- **Bear:** Critics cite unresolved legal, political and governance overhangs: bipartisan criticism of the reinstatement, an ongoing probe into reports Huang was a foreign-intelligence target, a delayed 10-Q, a reported SEC accounting review, no public answer to Treasury's export-control questions by the Nov. 25 deadline, the Megaspeed blacklisting, a CBS report on China-controlled U.S. partners, a new securities class action and congressional testimony from Dec. 1; S&P put the AA rating on CreditWatch negative over governance, the Motley Fool flags record CDS spreads at big AI customers and rising rate-hike odds, and the stock sits about 40% below its October record.
