## Bull case / bear case

- **Bull:** Q3 beat handily (revenue $115.8B vs. ~$108.9B expected, data center up 110%), the ~$130B Q4 guide topped consensus and the fiscal 2028 growth outlook rose to 75% even with no China data-center compute assumed; management calls the 71-72% gross margin a trough and says Vera Rubin is off to a strong start, even after cuts targets from Jefferies, Morgan Stanley, Wells Fargo and UBS ($220-260) sit well above the share price, Aptus Capital calls the franchise intact, and LSEG data put the forward P/E near 9.8 vs. about 31 for AMD.
- **Bear:** At least 10 analysts cut to Hold after Huang's exit and Summit Insights followed post-earnings, with Freedom Capital and others citing governance, policy and customer-financing risks; the board has ruled out Huang's return with no CEO timeline, the 10-Q is delayed by an internal investigation, the WSJ reports an SEC probe and the DOJ is examining the Megaspeed link, gross margin is guided down, and the stock has fallen six sessions in a row to $145.96, about 38% below its October record, even as Anthropic's debut failed to lift AI names.
