## Bull case / bear case

- **Bull:** Some analysts still rate the stock Buy despite cutting targets (Stifel at $205 says fundamentals are intact, Citi at $225 notes about 11x earnings near a prior trough, DA Davidson at $200), Goldman's James Schneider earlier called it a top pick, and the board says operations, customer commitments and the product roadmap continue under interim CEO Colette Kress, who has pledged to honor ~$164.5B of existing data-center commitments; Main Street Research's James Demmert calls the valuation attractive, and the Nov 17 report (guide ~$108B) is the next test of AI demand.
- **Bear:** Huang's abrupt Nov 12 exit drew downgrades to Hold from Bernstein, Truist and Craig-Hallum, an Oppenheimer downgrade with its target pulled, HSBC's Nov 13 cut to Hold ($170 target) (plus Baird's cut a day earlier), with analysts citing a hard-to-fill leadership gap, possible brain drain and cultural damage (Demmert also warns Nvidia could become less innovative without Huang); coverage flags China-bound shipments via Megaspeed, a shareholder suit, FBI, House and Senate scrutiny (Senate Banking hearings requested for Dec 3 and Dec 10), a possibly more cautious stance on new financing deals and a stock down about 13% from its Nov 6 close.
