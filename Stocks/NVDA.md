## Bull case / bear case

- **Bull:** Supporters point to Huang's unanimous reinstatement (with the board citing U.S. government assurances on security), which sent the stock up 31.5% on Nov. 23, plus a Q3 beat (revenue $115.8B, data center +110%), a ~$130B Q4 guide above consensus, a raised 75% fiscal 2028 growth outlook and management's view that the 71-72% gross margin is a trough; post-earnings targets from Jefferies, Morgan Stanley, Wells Fargo and UBS ($220-260) remain well above the share price, and the NYT says the SEC inquiry is early-stage and may end without charges.
- **Bear:** Critics cite unresolved legal and governance overhangs: an independent committee is still probing reports that Huang was a foreign-intelligence target, the 10-Q is delayed, the SEC is reportedly examining possible accounting issues, California's attorney general flags chip-diversion red flags, and Huang faces House interviews from Dec. 1; Freedom Capital and others also point to policy risk, large receivables and customer-financing guarantees, margins are guided lower, and even after Monday's jump the stock sits well below its October record.
