## Bull case / bear case

- **Bull:** Supporters (StoneX, TD Cowen, Rosenblatt Buy at $205) cite fast non-handset growth, with auto revenue up 89% to a record, an expected 60%+ rise in non-handset sales in fiscal 2027, a reiterated $5B fiscal-2027 data-center target with shipments to two hyperscalers, licensing renewals running to fiscal 2030, the Starlink Mobile 5G win and cost savings from job cuts.
- **Bear:** Cautious analysts (Barclays Sell at $150, Freedom Broker Hold at $185, Susquehanna at $165; UBS, JPMorgan, Goldman Sachs and Piper Sandler at Neutral) point to a 26% handset revenue drop, input-cost inflation and new growth bets squeezing margins, a shrinking Apple modem business and an EPS guide midpoint below consensus; MarketBeat's consensus is Hold.
