## Bull case / bear case

- **Bull:** Supporters (StoneX, TD Cowen, Rosenblatt Buy at $205, Daiwa at $200) cite fast non-handset growth: auto revenue rose 89% to a record $2B quarter, management targets $40B of non-handset QCT revenue in fiscal 2029, and it sees data center exiting fiscal 2027 at a $7B run rate; they also point to licensing renewals through fiscal 2030, savings from job cuts, a ~18x forward multiple a Motley Fool contributor calls low, and new executive awards tied to share-price hurdles up to $500 (per Investing.com).
- **Bear:** Cautious analysts (Barclays and BofA Sell, Bernstein Hold at $155, Freedom Capital Hold at $185; UBS, JPMorgan, Goldman Sachs and Piper Sandler Neutral) point to a 26% handset revenue drop, the Apple modem wind-down, memory-cost inflation and lower QCT margin guidance of 20-22%; Trefis notes data-center revenue rests on two customers, Baird cut its target over a lost ByteDance ASIC deal, the $4B notes sale added debt, and Investing.com flags exposure to proposed tariffs on China, and GuruFocus sees the stock near its GF Value; shares remain roughly 30% below their May peak despite gains on Nov. 25 and Nov. 27 (JournalArta, TradingKey).
