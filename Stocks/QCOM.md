## Bull case / bear case

- **Bull:** Supporters (StoneX, TD Cowen, Rosenblatt Buy at $205) cite fast non-handset growth, with auto revenue up 89% to a record, an expected 60%+ rise in non-handset sales in fiscal 2027, a data-center ramp management sees exiting fiscal 2027 at a $7B run rate, licensing renewals running to fiscal 2030, the Starlink Mobile 5G win and cost savings from job cuts; Daiwa has lifted its target to $200, and a Motley Fool contributor calls the ~18x forward multiple low relative to management's fiscal 2029 EPS goal above $18.
- **Bear:** Cautious analysts (Barclays Sell at $150, Freedom Broker Hold at $185, Susquehanna at $165; UBS, JPMorgan, Goldman Sachs and Piper Sandler at Neutral) point to a 26% handset revenue drop, input-cost inflation and new growth bets squeezing margins, a shrinking Apple modem business and an EPS guide midpoint below consensus; MarketBeat's consensus is Hold, TradingKey cites a consensus fiscal-2027 EPS cut to about $9.71 from $10.54, and the stock fell 3.6% on Nov 13 and was down again in premarket trading on Nov 16 amid a broad chip selloff.
