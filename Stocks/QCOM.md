## Bull case / bear case

- **Bull:** Supporters (StoneX, TD Cowen, Susquehanna) cite fast non-handset growth, with auto revenue up 89% to a record in fiscal Q4, a reiterated $5B fiscal-2027 data-center target with Amazon in production, a revenue beat and above-consensus revenue guidance, and the new Starlink Mobile 5G infrastructure win.
- **Bear:** Cautious analysts (Bernstein SocGen, Piper Sandler, RBC, Wells Fargo) point to a 26% handset revenue drop, rising memory and component costs, an EPS guidance midpoint below consensus, a shrinking Apple modem business and Samsung competition; shares fell about 5% after hours on Nov 4 and consensus ratings lean Hold.
