## Bull case / bear case

- **Bull:** Analysts (Bernstein $722, UBS and Goldman Sachs $700, Citi $680, Piper Sandler and Morgan Stanley $650, BNP Paribas $632, JPMorgan $625; averages near $637-$644 per Tradingpedia and Benzinga) cite 48% Azure growth with demand still above supply, Anthropic's $44.9B spending commitment and IPO stake, and clearer AI pricing from Ignite (seats plus metered Copilot Credits); BNP Paribas, JPMorgan and Guggenheim say its full stack, in-house MAI models and Maia/Cobalt chips can contain costs, and TradingKey framed the Nov. 25 drop as multiple compression rather than a break in its enterprise position.
- **Bear:** Skeptics cite a 12% drop in commercial bookings, capex heading toward ~$170B a year, falling free cash flow and a high free-cash-flow multiple (TradingKey), plus doubts over Copilot quality and usage (Guggenheim; BNP estimates ~8% seat penetration), with metered revenue mostly a 2027 story (JPMorgan); the 24-hour Microsoft 365 outage and resulting "Code Red" (Reuters), a ByteDance Azure deal under House China committee scrutiny, contingency planning for losing OpenAI access (Dataconomy via GuruFocus) and tariff exposure add risk, and shares trade below their 20-, 50- and 100-day averages (Benzinga).
