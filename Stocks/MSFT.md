## Bull case / bear case

- **Bull:** Analysts (UBS $700, Citi $680, Piper Sandler and Morgan Stanley $650, BNP Paribas $632, JPMorgan $625; consensus near $636 with no Sells per 24/7 Wall St.) cite 48% Azure growth with demand still above supply, Anthropic's $44.9B spending commitment and IPO stake, and clearer AI pricing from Ignite (seats plus metered Copilot Credits); BNP Paribas, JPMorgan and Guggenheim say Microsoft's full stack, in-house MAI models and Maia/Cobalt chips can contain costs, and BNP notes about 90% of Copilot customers let Microsoft route models automatically.
- **Bear:** Skeptics cite a 12% drop in commercial bookings, capex heading toward ~$170B a year, falling free cash flow and a high free-cash-flow multiple (TradingKey), plus doubts over Copilot quality and usage (Guggenheim; BNP estimates ~8% seat penetration), with metered revenue mostly a 2027 story (JPMorgan); rival Anthropic office tools, a reported ByteDance Azure deal that drew House China committee scrutiny, reported contingency planning for losing OpenAI access (Dataconomy via GuruFocus) and weak options positioning (Vanda) add risk, and shares lagged the Nov 23 rebound, rising 1.6% vs. the Nasdaq's 6.5%.
