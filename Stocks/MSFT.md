## Bull case / bear case

- **Bull:** Analysts (UBS, Wells Fargo, Citi, BofA, TD Cowen) raised targets after 48% Azure growth, ~49% Azure guidance and a free-cash-flow beat, with management saying demand still exceeds supply.
