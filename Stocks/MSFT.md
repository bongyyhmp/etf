## Bull case / bear case

- **Bull:** Analysts (UBS, Wells Fargo, Citi, BofA, TD Cowen) raised targets after 48% Azure growth and ~49% Azure guidance, with management saying demand still exceeds supply; Anthropic's $44.9B spending commitment plus a ~$1B stake in its IPO placement add visibility, and TradingKey cites Copilot adoption, an average target near $629 and targets from UBS ($700), TD Cowen ($650) and RBC ($640).
- **Bear:** Skeptics cite a 12% drop in commercial bookings, capex heading toward ~$170B a year, free cash flow down about 23% over four quarters (Motley Fool), a valuation near 66x free cash flow (TradingKey), reported pullbacks from neocloud and Oracle capacity deals, and new Anthropic office tools seen as a challenge to Microsoft 365, which helped push shares down ~2% on Nov 10; on Nov 11 the stock fell another ~2.3% as Benzinga also flagged a proposed US ban on Chinese-made data-center optical transceivers.
