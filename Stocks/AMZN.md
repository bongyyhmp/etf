## Bull case / bear case

- **Bull:** Bulls (UBS, Mizuho, MoffettNathanson, TD Cowen, Evercore, Phillip Securities and Freedom Broker) point to AWS growth accelerating to 39%, a $578B AWS backlog, Anthropic's $110B minimum AWS commitment and Andy Jassy's view (per the Motley Fool) that AWS could grow more than 40% in 2027 and 2028.
- **Bear:** Critics point to the Q3 EPS and advertising misses, a $42.4B trailing free-cash-flow outflow with capex near $220B, the ongoing FTC and state ad-pricing lawsuit, possible tougher data-center rules after the midterms, and target cuts from Citizens, Baird and BofA.
