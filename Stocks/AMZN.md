## Bull case / bear case

- **Bull:** Bulls (JPMorgan's Doug Anmuth naming it a top AI pick, plus UBS, Mizuho, MoffettNathanson, TD Cowen, Evercore and Freedom Broker) point to AWS growth accelerating to 39%, a $578B AWS backlog, Anthropic's $110B minimum AWS commitment plus an Anthropic stake a Reuters calculation values near $272B at the top of the IPO range, and guidance for AWS growth above 40% in 2027 and 2028.
- **Bear:** Critics point to the Q3 EPS and advertising misses, a $42.4B trailing free-cash-flow outflow with capex near $220B, the ongoing FTC and state ad-pricing lawsuit, possible tougher data-center rules after the midterms, target cuts from Citizens, Baird and BofA, and a share price GuruFocus puts slightly above its own fair-value estimate.
