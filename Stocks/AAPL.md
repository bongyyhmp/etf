## Bull case / bear case

- **Bull:** Analysts (China Renaissance at a Street-high $425, Wedbush $400, Bernstein $380, BofA $370) cite the fiscal Q4 beat (iPhone +17%, record $31.6B Services), 7%-10% December-quarter growth guidance and AI upside from a reworked Siri; Bernstein, TradingKey and the Motley Fool see the capital-light model (AI capex near 2% of revenue, ~$136B of free cash flow) as a relative haven, with shares roughly flat in November while the Nasdaq-100 fell about 15% and trading near a record; Counterpoint data show iPhone outperforming China's shrinking Double 11 market, and Bloomberg reports smart glasses are planned for 2027.
- **Bear:** Critics point to 45%-46% gross margin guidance (vs. 47.9% in fiscal Q4) squeezed by memory costs, a Greater China miss and a rich valuation (about 36x-39x trailing earnings per Trefis and 24/7 Wall St.; GuruFocus fair value near $299), with shares above the ~$341 average target; DZ Bank, Loop Capital and MoffettNathanson cut ratings or targets toward ~$280-$300 and Daiwa expects the outperformance to fade, as on Nov 23 when Apple rose just 1.9% versus the Nasdaq's 6.5%; memory supply may cap Mac and iPad sales, new US tariffs could hit iPhone-assembly countries, South Korea's regulator is weighing action over iPhone 16 AI advertising, and the Nov 17 iCloud and Apple Pay outage raised reliability questions.
