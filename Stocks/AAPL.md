## Bull case / bear case

- **Bull:** Analysts (China Renaissance at a Street-high $425, Wedbush $400, Bernstein $380, BofA $370) cite the fiscal Q4 beat (iPhone +17%, record $31.6B Services), 7%-10% December-quarter growth guidance and possible AI upside from a reworked Siri; Bernstein and TradingKey see the capital-light model (AI capex near 2% of revenue, ~$136B of free cash flow) as a relative haven, which showed on Nov 20 when shares fell about 1.9% versus a 5.3% Nasdaq drop, and Bloomberg reports smart glasses are planned for 2027.
- **Bear:** Critics point to 45%-46% gross margin guidance squeezed by memory costs, a Greater China miss and a rich valuation (about 36x-39x trailing earnings per Trefis and 24/7 Wall St.; GuruFocus fair value near $299), with shares above the ~$341 average target; DZ Bank, Loop Capital and MoffettNathanson cut ratings or targets toward ~$280-$300 and Daiwa expects the outperformance to fade, which showed on Nov 23 when Apple gained only 1.9% as the Nasdaq jumped 6.5% (it then rose 1.4% on Nov 24 as the Nasdaq fell). Memory supply may cap Mac and iPad sales, and the Nov 17 iCloud and Apple Pay outage raised reliability questions.
