## Bull case / bear case

- **Bull:** Analysts (Bernstein, Argus, Evercore ISI and Redburn in the $360-$400 range; Wedbush $400; BofA's Wamsi Mohan reiterating Buy) cite the fiscal Q4 beat (iPhone +17%, record Services), December-quarter revenue guidance of 7%-10% growth, strong cash generation and possible AI upside from a reworked Siri; shares posted a first-ever close above $350 on Nov 9.
- **Bear:** Critics point to 45%-46% December-quarter gross margin guidance hurt by memory costs, a Greater China miss and a rich valuation, with shares above the ~$341 average target (24/7 Wall St.); DZ Bank, Loop Capital and MoffettNathanson cut ratings or targets toward ~$280-$300, JPMorgan trimmed to $355, and Daiwa (per CNBC) expects Apple's outperformance to fade.
