## Bull case / bear case

- **Bull:** Analysts (China Renaissance, upgrading to Buy with a Street-high $425 target; Bernstein, Argus, Evercore ISI and Redburn in the $360-$400 range; Wedbush $400; BofA's Wamsi Mohan reiterating Buy) cite the fiscal Q4 beat (iPhone +17%, record Services), December-quarter revenue guidance of 7%-10% growth and possible AI upside from a reworked Siri; TradingKey highlights a capital-light model (~$136B free cash flow on ~$9B capex) that drew money during the AI-chip selloff, shares set a record close of $356.27 on Nov 13 after a 4%+ week, and Bloomberg reports smart glasses are planned for 2027.
- **Bear:** Critics point to 45%-46% December-quarter gross margin guidance hurt by memory costs, a Greater China miss and a rich valuation (about 36x forward earnings per the Motley Fool; GuruFocus fair value near $299), with shares above the ~$341 average target (24/7 Wall St.); DZ Bank, Loop Capital and MoffettNathanson cut ratings or targets toward ~$280-$300, JPMorgan trimmed to $355, Daiwa (per CNBC) expects the outperformance to fade, the reported glasses software is behind schedule, and shares eased about 0.8% at midday on Nov 16 as tech sold off.
