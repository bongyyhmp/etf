## Bull case / bear case

- **Bull:** Analysts (China Renaissance, upgrading to Buy with a Street-high $425 target; Bernstein, Argus, Evercore ISI and Redburn in the $360-$400 range; Wedbush $400) cite the fiscal Q4 beat (iPhone +17%, record Services), December-quarter revenue guidance of 7%-10% growth and possible AI upside from a reworked Siri; TradingKey highlights a capital-light model (~$136B free cash flow on ~$9B capex) that has made the stock a relative haven in the AI-chip selloff, 24/7 Wall St. notes it is the only Magnificent Seven stock near a record (up about 29% this year), and Bloomberg reports smart glasses are planned for 2027.
- **Bear:** Critics point to 45%-46% December-quarter gross margin guidance hurt by memory costs, a Greater China miss and a rich valuation (about 36x-39x trailing earnings per Trefis and 24/7 Wall St., versus about 28x for Microsoft; GuruFocus fair value near $299), with consensus already assuming thinner margins and possible Mac/iPad supply limits (Trefis); shares sit above the ~$341 average target (24/7 Wall St.), DZ Bank, Loop Capital and MoffettNathanson cut ratings or targets toward ~$280-$300, Daiwa (per CNBC) expects the outperformance to fade, the reported glasses software is behind schedule, and a multi-hour iCloud and Apple Pay outage on Nov 17, which Apple blamed on an internal update, raised reliability questions.
