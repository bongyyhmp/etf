## Recent news

- **2026-10-29** — Shares fell roughly 16-17% after hours as the Q4 outlook disappointed: Intel guided revenue to $15B-$16B (vs. about $17B expected), non-GAAP EPS of $0.25 and gross margin falling to about 37%, citing rising memory costs from the global chip shortage. ([source](https://en.fnnews.com/news/202610300514093044))

([source](https://panews.io/articles/01a1895c-e6d5-7730-9080-17e1515efd50))
- **2026-10-29** — Ahead of the report, the stock closed the regular session down about 3.7% at $92.03. ([source](https://www.marketbeat.com/earnings/reports/2026-10-29-intel-co-stock/))
- **2026-10-28** — Shares were flat near $93.73 the day before results; analysts expect about $0.39 EPS on $16.45B revenue, and the consensus rating is Hold with an average target near $115. ## Bull case / bear case

- **Bull:** New Street (Buy, $125) and KeyBanc (Overweight, $155) cite tight server-CPU supply from AI demand and the Oracle cloud deal; Q3 showed 21% revenue growth, an EPS beat versus ~$0.40 consensus and the strongest data-center growth in 15 years.
- **Bear:** Q4 guidance came in well below estimates with gross margin seen near 37%, memory-cost pressure and a PC slowdown expected into mid-2027; Needham (Hold), RBC (Sector Perform) and Rosenblatt (Sell, $80) were already cautious.
