## Bull case / bear case

- **Bull:** Coverage cites a seventh straight EPS beat, 5% unit-case volume growth, guidance raised to at least 11% comparable EPS growth for 2026, post-results target raises from JPMorgan, UBS, Wells Fargo, Citi and TD Cowen, and record highs as investors rotated into defensive staples after the midterms (Motley Fool, 24/7 Wall St.).
- **Bear:** Commentators flag a valuation near 28-29x earnings, well above its ~24x five-year average (a Nov 9 Motley Fool column says the price already bakes in years of growth), flat North America volume, a shorter Q4 with destocking and the pending CCBA sale, an unresolved IRS tax case (Trefis), tougher 2027 comparisons (UBS) and recent executive share sales.
