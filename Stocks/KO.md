## Bull case / bear case

- **Bull:** Coverage cites a seventh straight EPS beat, 5% unit-case volume growth, guidance raised to at least 11% comparable EPS growth for 2026, post-results target raises from JPMorgan, UBS, Wells Fargo, Citi and TD Cowen, 22 straight quarters of value-share gains, and record highs (a record close of $97.57 on Nov 11, then a close of $99.25 on Nov 13 as the Dow's top gainer, up about 40% year to date) as investors rotated into defensive staples; targets cited include UBS ($106, Buy), TD Cowen ($104) and Morgan Stanley ($103) (Motley Fool, 24/7 Wall St., Zacks, Investing.com, Techfocus24).
- **Bear:** Commentators flag a valuation near 28-29x earnings, well above its ~24x five-year average (a Nov 9 Motley Fool column says the price already bakes in years of growth), flat North America volume, a shorter Q4 with destocking and the pending CCBA sale, an unresolved IRS tax case (Trefis), tougher 2027 comparisons (UBS; Zacks notes consensus sees 2027 revenue slightly lower), a price now above the ~$97 average analyst target (MarketBeat) and Bernstein's $95 (Neutral, reiterated Nov 13), a Zacks Hold rank and recent executive share sales.
