- **Bull:** Coverage cites a seventh straight EPS beat, 5% unit-case volume growth, guidance for at least 11% comparable EPS growth and $13B+ of free cash flow in 2026, 64 years of dividend increases, post-results target raises (TD Cowen to $112, JPMorgan, UBS, Wells Fargo, Citi), Wolfe Research's new Outperform ($110) citing share gains from PepsiCo, an unchanged Berkshire stake, a 2026 gain of about 40% and its appeal as a defensive holding during market selloffs (Motley Fool); 24/7 Wall St. counts 20 Buy or Strong Buy ratings.
- **Bear:** Commentators flag a valuation Barron's calls the highest in 25 years (about 32x trailing and 28-30x forward earnings, versus ~24x on a five-year average), a 2-3% drop on Nov 19, an ~8% 2027 revenue headwind from divestitures including the African bottler sale (24/7 Wall St.), flat North America volume and share losses in France and the UK, an unresolved IRS tax case, a price above several average targets (~$97-99) and Bernstein's $95 (Neutral), a yield near 2.2% below Treasury yields, a Zacks Hold rank and recent executive share sales.
