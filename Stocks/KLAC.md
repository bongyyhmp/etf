## Bull case / bear case

- **Bull:** Trefis cites accelerating sales tied to leading-edge AI chips and advanced packaging, roughly 12-month lead times, its best operating margin since 2017 (45% in the September quarter) and December-quarter revenue guided to about $4.7B; S&P Global upgraded its credit rating to A on AI demand and low leverage, forecasting ~39% fiscal 2027 revenue growth, and Nvidia's Nov 17 beat-and-raise ($115.8B revenue, $130B Q4 guide) supported AI-capex sentiment, with KLA up about 1.8% after hours.
- **Bear:** Trefis notes high memory costs squeezing margins, some customers delaying deliveries until fabs are ready, a gross margin barely moved in five quarters with the 63%-64% target not expected until mid-2027 or later, and a valuation (~38x trailing earnings) well above the S&P 500's; KLA fell 3.8% on Nov 13 and 5.3% on Nov 16 (to $144.10) as equipment names sold off after Applied's report, and chip stocks remain sensitive to Nvidia's leadership uncertainty and new US export curbs, with Nvidia closing down about 2.9% on Nov 18 despite its beat (its guide assumes no China data-center sales) while KLA was roughly flat.
