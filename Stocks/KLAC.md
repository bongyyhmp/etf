## Bull case / bear case

- **Bull:** Trefis cites accelerating sales tied to leading-edge AI chips and advanced packaging (September-quarter revenue up about 29% to $4.14B, December quarter guided to about $4.7B), roughly 12-month lead times and its best operating margin since 2017 (45%); S&P Global upgraded its credit rating to A on AI demand and low leverage, the new Singapore expansion adds packaging and silicon-photonics capacity, and Finexus reported an Evercore ISI upgrade to Outperform on Nov. 27 alongside a proposed CHIPS tax-credit extension.
- **Bear:** The stock swings with AI-spending sentiment (roughly -9% to -10% on Nov. 20, +14% on Nov. 23, -7% to -8% on Nov. 24, an early ~7% drop on Nov. 25 that mostly faded, and about +5% on Nov. 27); Finexus notes it was still about 20% below its Oct. 28 post-earnings level; Trefis notes high memory costs, some customers delaying deliveries until fabs are ready, a gross margin (61.9%) flat for five quarters and a valuation near 38x trailing earnings, GuruFocus puts its GF Value below the share price, and 24/7 Wall St. flags MATCH Act risk to China sales.
