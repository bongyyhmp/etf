# Market Scan: Tue, Nov 10, 2026

> [!summary] The day in one line
> A hotter-than-expected October CPI reading pushed the 10-year Treasury yield to about 5% and firmed up bets on a December Fed rate hike, so stocks gave back a slice of Monday's big rally: the S&P 500 slipped 0.38% and the Nasdaq 0.74%, with [[NVDA]] down another 5.5% as its CEO controversy widened — while [[AMD]] and [[AVGO]] kept climbing.

## What moved the market

- **Inflation ran hot.** October CPI rose 0.4% for the month (0.3% expected; September was 0.6%) and core rose 0.3% (0.2% expected). The release had no year-over-year figures because of data gaps from last year's government shutdown.
- **Rate-hike talk got louder.** Fed Governor Christopher Waller said he favors a quarter-point hike in December and that a half-point move could be warranted after a hot core reading; futures priced roughly an 84% chance of 25 bp and about 15% of 50 bp. The 10-year yield climbed to around 5%.
- **Nvidia fell again; other chips split.** [[NVDA]] closed down 5.47% at $168.89, its lowest close since March 30, as The Information reported billions of dollars of its hardware reached China through a network tied to the woman linked to CEO Jensen Huang, a shareholder suit was filed and President Trump said the FBI is looking into it. Money kept rotating to other chipmakers: [[AMD]] closed up 5.0% and [[AVGO]] 3.7%, and [[QCOM]] added 1.2%, while [[MU]], [[MRVL]] and [[INTC]] fell about 3% intraday. Goldman's James Schneider called chip stocks oversold and named Nvidia, Broadcom and AMD among his top picks.
- **Defensives held up.** Coverage described a rotation out of AI names into sectors such as healthcare; oil eased again after Trump said he'd hold off on new Iran strikes.

## Notable company news

- **Anthropic IPO terms:** an amended filing set a $70–$78 range for 825M shares (about $1.3–1.45T valuation), with trading expected Nov 19 or 20. [[NVDA]], [[MSFT]], [[GOOGL]], [[AVGO]], [[AMD]], [[MU]] and [[TSM]] are buying a combined $26B of its shares in a private placement; Google is adding another $10B, and the filing shows [[AMZN]] with a larger share count than Google.
- **Microsoft ([[MSFT]])** fell about 2% after Anthropic launched spreadsheet, whiteboard and data tools seen as Office rivals.
- **TSMC ([[TSM]])** posted record October revenue of NT$586.89B, up 59.7% from a year earlier, and its board approved up to $500M each for stakes in Anthropic and OpenAI.
- **Apple ([[AAPL]])** slipped about 0.9% at midday after Monday's first-ever close above $350; [[AMZN]] gained about 0.7% and [[META]] held Monday's jump; [[TSLA]] eased after a 5.5% gain.
- **Retail and healthcare:** UBS and Mizuho raised [[WMT]] targets ahead of its Nov 19 report; Bernstein lifted its [[HD]] target before its Nov 17 report; [[JNJ]] said Caplyta succeeded in a second Phase 3 bipolar mania trial; the FDA accepted two [[MRK]] HIV filings; [[ABT]] won FDA approval for a new defibrillation lead; [[UNH]] reportedly had a $28-a-share bid rejected by Surgery Partners.
- **Energy:** [[XOM]] signed a preliminary deal with Venezuela's PDVSA to assess a return, and QatarEnergy agreed to take 25% of [[CVX]]'s offshore Namibia licence; [[COP]] and the majors remain below their Oct 30 highs after a rough election week.
- **Other movers (not in the vault):** Hims & Hers fell as much as 15% on a weak revenue outlook; Coinbase and Strategy fell about 4%; CoreWeave reported after the close.
