# Market Scan: Tue, Oct 27, 2026

> [!summary] The day in one line
> Chip stocks pushed the Nasdaq up more than 1% for a second straight gain, while the Dow barely moved as Boeing slumped; investors held positions ahead of Wednesday's Fed decision and big tech earnings.

## What moved the market

- **Chips and AI kept rallying.** Semiconductors led again as Anthropic's IPO filing from Monday kept the AI trade going. [[NVDA]] said it will invest $3B in [[META]]'s stock offering as part of a broader AI partnership, and [[AVGO]] rose about 3.5% early after a D.A. Davidson upgrade.
- **Fed waiting game.** The Fed's two-day meeting began, with a decision due Wednesday. Futures pricing implied only about a 10% chance of a rate hike.
- **Oil eased.** WTI crude settled near $100 a barrel, down about 1.7%, on hopes of calmer Middle East tensions; Brent slipped below $110. The 10-year Treasury yield held near 4.99%.

## Notable company news

- **Coca-Cola ([[KO]])** beat estimates (adjusted EPS $0.91 vs. about $0.88; revenue about $13.4B, up 7%) and raised its 2026 outlook again, but slower North American volume weighed and shares fell about 1.6%.
- **NXP ([[NXPI]])** posted Q3 revenue up about 19% to $3.79B, slightly above estimates, but its Q4 revenue outlook was a touch light and shares dropped about 9% right after the report.
- **Intel ([[INTC]])** rose about 3.5% after a New Street upgrade and its deal to buy quantum-software firm Classiq for about $2.2B.
- **Apple ([[AAPL]])** edged up after introducing a new iMac and 14-inch MacBook Pro.
- **Other movers (not in the vault):** Boeing fell about 10% midday after cutting its delivery target; SoFi jumped about 13% on results and raised guidance; UPS gained about 2% on higher revenue guidance; Cintas slid on reports the FTC plans to challenge its UniFirst deal; Oracle was reported to be adding AI chips from Google, Cerebras and SambaNova.
