# Market Scan: Mon, Nov 23, 2026

> [!summary] The day in one line
> Wall Street had its best day since April 2025 after [[NVDA]]'s board reinstated Jensen Huang as CEO: Nvidia soared about 31%, the Nasdaq jumped 6.5%, the S&P 500 4.7% (snapping a seven-day losing streak) and the Dow 3.0%, while the VIX fell about 26%, oil dropped more than 3% and defensive names like [[KO]] and [[UNH]] slipped.

## What moved the market

- **Huang is back.** Nvidia's board unanimously reinstated Jensen Huang as president, CEO and chair, effective immediately, eleven days after he resigned. The board said senior U.S. government officials had told it his return poses no national-security risk, and that its independent investigation will still run to completion (Yahoo Finance). AP noted President Trump had publicly pushed for the move. None of the coverage read addressed the SEC accounting review reported on Friday.
- **AI and chips roared back.** [[NVDA]] closed up 31.5% at $165.05, the S&P 500's top gainer, followed by Dell (+20.5%, not in the vault) and [[MU]] (+19.0%). [[MRVL]] gained about 18%, [[MPWR]] about 17%, and [[INTC]], [[TSM]], [[AMAT]] and [[KLAC]] rose 13–14%. Oracle (not in the vault) added about 11% after saying funding for its OpenAI contract is in place (AP).
- **Breadth was strong.** Advancers beat decliners by about 5 to 1 on the NYSE and nearly 4 to 1 on the Nasdaq. Tech, industrials and materials led (Investing.com).
- **Defensives and energy lagged.** Money rotated out of last week's havens: [[KO]] was the Dow's worst performer (-1.1%), and oil's 3–4% slide pulled [[COP]] (-2.9%), [[CVX]] (-1.1%) and [[XOM]] lower. Bitcoin rebounded to about $63,000 after a Saturday low near $55,100 (Yahoo Finance).
- **Fed still expected to hike.** Prediction and futures markets still put roughly 90% odds on a quarter-point hike at the Dec 8–9 meeting (Yahoo Finance). Today's flash PMI readings and New York Fed President Williams's remarks were not reported in the coverage read.

## Notable company news

- **Chips:** [[AMD]] rose 6.9% to $453.46 and [[AVGO]] about 3%, lagging peers on a report that Google cut a TPU order. [[TXN]] gained 11.8% after a Cantor upgrade, and [[ADI]] rose about 10% ahead of Tuesday's earnings, with Cantor and Evercore raising targets. [[ASML]] gained about 8.9% in Amsterdam. [[QCOM]] rose about 10%.
- **Megacaps:** [[TSLA]] +11.5%, [[META]] +6.6% (ending a five-day slide), [[GOOGL]] +6.3%, [[AMZN]] +3.5%, [[AAPL]] +1.9% and [[MSFT]] +1.6%. The FT reported Amazon is in advanced talks to buy AI startup Decart for about $7.5B. [[PLTR]] gained about 11%.
- **Software and security:** CrowdStrike (not in the vault) agreed to buy Zscaler for about $43.9B in stock and fell about 8.9%, the S&P 500's worst performer; [[PANW]] rose about 3.4%. [[CSCO]] gained 6.8%, the Dow's No. 3 gainer.
- **Health care:** [[UNH]] fell 1.1% to $336.08 after CMS proposed a Medicare Advantage star-ratings overhaul late Friday; Elevance (not in the vault) dropped 3.8%. [[LLY]] edged up 1.3%, and the FDA approved AbbVie's Rinvoq for vitiligo.
- **Other:** [[COP]] agreed to sell its Norway business and a UK terminal stake to Vår Energi for a headline $4.4B. Richard McPhail took over as [[HD]] CEO, and Guggenheim trimmed its target. [[WMT]] gained about 1.3% (close unconfirmed). Anthropic (ANT) slipped 1.4% to $87.64.
