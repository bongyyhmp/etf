# Market Scan: Thu, Nov 12, 2026

> [!summary] The day in one line
> Jensen Huang's sudden resignation as [[NVDA]] CEO jolted the AI trade — Nvidia fell as much as 13–14% before closing down 4.3% — while a 10-year yield back above 5% and a 1.7% drop in small caps kept the S&P 500 (-0.35%) and Nasdaq (-0.39%) slightly lower; after the bell [[AMAT]] and [[CSCO]] both beat and guided above forecasts.

## What moved the market

- **Nvidia lost its founder-CEO.** The board said Jensen Huang resigned effective immediately, following its probe into his conduct; CFO Colette Kress is interim CEO and the board named its first chair. [[NVDA]] closed at $166.19 (-4.34%) after a much deeper intraday drop, and Bernstein, Truist, Craig-Hallum and Oppenheimer moved to Hold or equivalent. Its Nov 17 earnings report still stands.
- **Chip stocks split.** Rivals gained on the turmoil — [[AMD]] (~+5.9%, with a William Blair upgrade) and [[AVGO]] (~+5.5%, a fourth straight gain) — while Nvidia partners and suppliers slipped: [[MU]] (~-1.6%), [[INTC]], [[MRVL]] and [[TSM]] ADRs traded lower; [[QCOM]] rose about 3%.
- **Yields and the Fed.** The 10-year yield climbed to about 5.1% as traders kept leaning toward a December rate hike after Tuesday's hot CPI; existing-home sales fell 5.5% in October and weekly jobless claims rose to 200,000.
- **Oil jumped, gold and bitcoin fell.** Crude rose nearly 3% after President Trump said the U.S. doesn't need an Iran deal, overriding an unexpected 4.7M-barrel EIA crude build; gold dropped about 3% and bitcoin slid below $72,000.

## Notable company news

- **Earnings after the bell:** [[AMAT]] posted record fiscal Q4 revenue of $10.41B (+53%) and $4.16 adjusted EPS, both above estimates, and guided fiscal Q1 to about $11.1B, above consensus; the stock had fallen 3.6% into the report. [[CSCO]] beat with $18.5B revenue (+24%), raised its fiscal 2027 outlook to $74.8–75.8B and lifted its hyperscaler AI order target to $9B; early after-hours moves for both were positive but unsettled.
- **Megacaps:** [[GOOGL]] rose about 1.8% and [[AMZN]] about 0.5% as coverage pointed to their in-house AI chips; [[MSFT]] traded higher ahead of next week's Ignite conference; [[META]] slipped about 0.8% and was fined £450,000 by UK regulator Ofcom; [[AAPL]] was roughly flat after its record. [[TSLA]] rose about 1.7% on a Bloomberg report that SpaceX–Tesla merger talks are progressing.
- **Software and security:** [[PANW]] fell 4.2% to $320.34 after Jefferies cut its target to $400 ahead of the Nov 18 report; a [[PLTR]] executive filed to sell about 300,000 shares under a pre-set plan.
- **Energy:** [[CVX]] rose 1.4% to $216.35, among the Dow's best, with Reuters reporting new Venezuela agreements expected Monday; Bernstein downgraded [[COP]] to Market-Perform and trimmed its [[XOM]] target.
- **Healthcare:** [[JNJ]] closed at a record $283.52 (+1.1%); [[LLY]] won Chinese approval for its weight-loss pill Foundayo and broke ground on a $3.5B Pennsylvania plant; Argus raised its [[AMGN]] target to $515.
- **Other:** [[WMT]] set its Q3 release for 6 a.m. CT on Nov 19; [[BRK-B]] traded near its 52-week high.
- **Not in the vault:** CoreWeave and Nebius gave back part of Wednesday's surge; MSCI said it will drop Strategy from two indexes; ADI Global Distribution (not Analog Devices) plunged about 48% after cutting its outlook.
