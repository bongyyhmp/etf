# Market Scan: Tue, Nov 24, 2026

> [!summary] The day in one line

## What moved the market

- **Chip rally cooled.** After Monday's 14% jump in the chip index, semiconductors fell back: [[MU]] -11.0%, [[AMD]] -8.9%, [[INTC]] -5% to -8% (sources differ), [[TXN]] -7.6%, [[KLAC]] -6.8%, [[NVDA]] -5.9% to $155.38, [[AMAT]] -5.3% and [[AVGO]] -4.0%. The Motley Fool put Nvidia's drop down mostly to profit-taking plus bipartisan criticism of Jensen Huang's reinstatement (Stock Titan, Motley Fool).
- **AI hardware names led the losers.** In 24/7 Wall St.'s tally, ADI was the day's biggest S&P 500 decliner, followed by HPE (-17.1%), Astera Labs (-14.3%), Dell (-12.0%) and CoreWeave (-11.9%) (none but ADI in the vault). Anthropic (ANT) closed at $81.91, below its $83 IPO price.
- **Rotation into defensives.** Health care led the sectors: [[AMGN]] (+5.5%) and [[MRK]] (+4.5%) were the Dow's second- and third-best performers behind Nike, with [[ABBV]] +3.5%, [[LLY]] +3.3% and [[JNJ]] +2.6%. Sanofi agreed to buy Insmed for about $41B, pharma's largest deal in three years (CNBC). Retailers rallied on upbeat reports from Dollar Tree and Best Buy, lifting [[WMT]] 2.1%.
- **Oil bounced from three-month lows** after UKMTO said three ships were struck by projectiles in the Strait of Hormuz; WTI rose 2.35% (Investrade). [[CVX]] traded up roughly 1–2% intraday.
- **Fed hike still expected.** Traders priced roughly 95% odds of a quarter-point hike at the Dec 8–9 meeting (Yahoo Finance). The consumer-confidence reading was not reported in the coverage read.
- **Overseas:** Taiwan's Taiex soared 8.99% to a record 40,122.35 as it caught up with Monday's U.S. rally, with [[TSM]] hitting its 10% daily limit; the Dutch AEX fell 1.07% and [[ASML]] about 4.7% (Focus Taiwan, Reuters via Ad-hoc News).

## Notable company news

- **[[ADI]]:** Record fiscal Q4 (revenue +42% to $4.37B, adjusted EPS $4.06, both above estimates), but a fiscal Q1 guide of $4.4B and $3.82 EPS was roughly flat with Q4 and its EPS midpoint sat below consensus; shares closed down 17.3% at $313.16.
- **[[MU]]:** BNP Paribas cut it to Neutral and its target to $775 from $1,700, warning that memory supply could catch up with demand by mid-2027.
- **[[AMD]]:** Registered about 337M shares for resale, including up to 160M each tied to OpenAI and Meta warrants; AMD receives no proceeds.
- **[[MSFT]]:** Reuters reported a "Code Red" internal push on Microsoft 365 reliability after the Nov 12–13 outage.
- **[[META]]:** Morgan Stanley kept Overweight but cut its target to $720 from $820; shares fell 1.3%. [[AAPL]] rose 1.4% to $354.07, one of few large tech gainers; [[AMZN]] -1.2%, [[GOOGL]] -1.1%, [[TSLA]] -0.2%.
- **[[JPM]]:** J.P. Morgan strategists set a year-end 2027 S&P 500 target of 8,200. [[CSCO]] fell 2.9%, the Dow's second-worst after Nvidia; [[BRK-B]] rose 0.85%.
- **[[KO]]:** Named Bristol Myers Squibb's David Elkins as CFO from April 2027, succeeding John Murphy, who retires.
- **[[LLY]]:** Retatrutide's sleep-apnea results were published in the New England Journal of Medicine.
