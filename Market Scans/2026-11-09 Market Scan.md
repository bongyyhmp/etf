# Market Scan: Mon, Nov 9, 2026

> [!summary] The day in one line
> Stocks had their best day since April as the AI and chip trade roared back from last week's rout: the S&P 500 jumped 2.35%, the Nasdaq 2.74% and small caps 3.2%, while the VIX fell about 22% and oil slid again — but [[NVDA]] fell about 4% after its board opened a probe into CEO Jensen Huang.

## What moved the market

- **The AI trade bounced back.** After a post-election week that pushed the Nasdaq into correction territory, chip stocks led a broad rally: [[AMD]] closed up about 13% after an Oppenheimer upgrade, [[AVGO]] nearly 9%, [[MRVL]] about 9%, [[AMAT]] 8.5%, [[INTC]] about 6%, [[ASML]] about 6%, [[KLAC]] 5.2%, [[MU]] 5.2%, [[QCOM]] 4.0% and [[TSM]] ADRs 3.9%.
- **Nvidia went the other way.** [[NVDA]] closed down 4.3% at $178.67 (it was down about 8% earlier) after its board launched an independent investigation into Huang's relationship with a woman US officials suspect of spying for China; analysts described the damage as mostly limited to Nvidia.
- **Analog chips joined in.** [[ADI]] rose about 5% and [[TXN]] about 4.5% with no company-specific news.
- **Oil kept falling.** Brent settled near $92.6 (about -5.9%) and WTI near $87 after President Trump called renewed strikes on Iran "highly unlikely"; energy was the only lagging sector, weighing on [[XOM]], [[CVX]] and [[COP]].
- **A note of caution.** Citi cut its view on US stocks and global tech to neutral even as markets rallied.

## Notable company news

- **Alphabet ([[GOOGL]])** unveiled two new AI chips, TPU 8tx and TPU 8i Max, due next year; shares rose only about 1.2%, while partners [[AVGO]] (TPU design) and [[AMD]] (host CPUs) benefited.
- **Meta ([[META]])** rose about 5% after JPMorgan added it to its Analyst Focus List with a $940 target; JPMorgan also highlighted [[AMZN]] (about +2.9%) as a top AI pick on expected AWS growth.
- **Tesla ([[TSLA]])** gained about 5.5% as Elon Musk said Cybertruck output ends in about eight months and many deposits will be refunded; [[PLTR]] rose about 6% and hired former Sen. Jon Tester as an adviser.
- **Berkshire ([[BRK-B]])** slipped about 1% after Saturday's Q3 report showed operating earnings down 5.8% on weaker insurance, offset by stronger BNSF results and about $9.6B of buybacks.
- **Healthcare:** [[LLY]] rose about 4.5% after Foundayo sleep-apnea trial data and a Street-high $1,700 Jefferies target; [[JNJ]] hit a record high; [[MRK]] reported positive mid-stage MASH data; [[AMGN]] presented new Repatha analyses.
- **Ahead of earnings:** [[CSCO]] rose about 3.8% and [[PANW]] about 3.1% ahead of their reports; JPMorgan raised its [[WMT]] target to $145 before its Nov 19 results.
- **Other movers (not in the vault):** Rocket Lab headed for a roughly 30% two-day gain on NASA Moon Base awards; Hims & Hers fell about 10% before its results.
