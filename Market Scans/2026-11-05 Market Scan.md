# Market Scan: Thu, Nov 5, 2026

> [!summary] The day in one line
> Wall Street erased Wednesday's election rally and then some: an AI and chip rout sent the Nasdaq down 5.5% into correction territory and the S&P 500 down 4.4%, its worst day since April 2025, as investors priced in a slower, costlier data-center buildout under a Democratic Congress; the semiconductor index lost about 12% and the VIX jumped about 70%.

## What moved the market

- **AI trade unwound.** Chip and AI stocks fell for a second day on worries that Democratic wins, local opposition to data centers and rising power prices will slow the buildout; UBS strategists estimated about 30% of planned 2028 data-center additions are now at risk, and Goldman put the capacity potentially exposed to moratoria near 45%.
- **Chips crashed.** [[MU]] fell about 17.5%, [[AMD]] about 17.3%, [[MRVL]] about 16.4%, [[AVGO]] about 10.4%, [[INTC]] about 10.3% and [[NVDA]] about 9.3% (now below its end-2025 close); [[TSM]] ADRs lost roughly 9–10% and [[ASML]] about 8%.
- **Profit-taking, not fundamentals?** Strategists, including Nomura's Charlie McElligott, described much of the move as hedge funds locking in 2026 gains; retail investors were heavy net sellers, the most since 2020.
- **Selling spread.** Every S&P 500 sector fell; software and AI-linked names such as [[PLTR]] (about -10%) and [[PANW]] (about -7.4%) dropped, and [[TSLA]] sank about 10.8% with SpaceX down about 9% on regulatory worries.
- **Megacaps fell less.** [[MSFT]] lost about 3.6%, [[META]] about 4%, [[GOOGL]] and [[AMZN]] about 2.2% each, and [[AAPL]] about 1.5%.
- **Oil and yields.** WTI rose about 3.2% to $90.96 on uncertainty over a possible Iran deal, after Wednesday's plunge; the 10-year Treasury yield fell to about 4.84%.
- **Rates abroad and at home.** The Bank of England raised its rate to 4% from 3.75%; futures put the odds of a December Fed hike near 70%, down from about 83% a day earlier.

## Notable company news

- **ConocoPhillips ([[COP]])** reported Q3 adjusted EPS of $3.27 on about $20.1B of revenue, both ahead of forecasts; production dipped year over year as Qatar output was disrupted, the dividend was raised 11% to $0.93, and Q4 output guidance was 2.32–2.36 million boe/d.
- **Qualcomm ([[QCOM]])** fell about 9.5% after its report showed a 26% drop in handset chip sales; Susquehanna and Rosenblatt cut targets.
- **Cisco ([[CSCO]])** fell about 5.9% as Morgan Stanley was reported to have cut it to Equal-Weight ahead of its Nov 12 report.
- **Apple ([[AAPL]])** sold about $7B of bonds, drawing roughly $31B of orders, per Bloomberg via TradingKey.
- **Berkshire ([[BRK-B]])** rose nearly 1% to a record as investors sought safety ahead of Saturday's results; **Lilly ([[LLY]])** touched an all-time high early before turning lower.
- **Banks and insurers slipped:** [[JPM]] fell about 3.5% and [[UNH]] about 3.3%; [[AMGN]] dropped about 2% even as several brokers raised targets.
- **Energy majors** [[XOM]] and [[CVX]] barely moved as crude rebounded; Iraq agreed key terms with Chevron for the West Qurna-2 field.
- **Other movers (not in the vault):** AppLovin jumped about 31% on strong results; Arm fell about 13%; MNTN and Veracyte each plunged about 55% after cutting forecasts.
