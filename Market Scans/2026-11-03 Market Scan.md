## What moved the market

- **Chips and big tech rebounded.** After Monday's slide, which put the Nasdaq-100 more than 10% below its Oct 6 record, [[NVDA]] rose about 2.3% (the biggest boost to the S&P 500) and [[AVGO]] about 3.1%; [[MSFT]], [[AMZN]] and [[META]] each gained more than 2%. [[MU]], [[INTC]], [[ADI]] and [[TXN]] also rose.
- **Apple dragged on the Dow.** [[AAPL]] lost about 1.9% as its holiday-quarter margin forecast, squeezed by higher memory-chip costs, overshadowed Monday's earnings beat.
- **Election Day.** Voting in the midterms was under way, with results expected through the evening; analysts and polls favored Democrats to take the House, with the Senate seen as uncertain.
- **Oil whipsawed.** Brent swung between roughly $101 and $107 after reports of a US strike on an Iranian position on Qeshm Island, then settled about 1.1% lower near $103.60.
- **Yields rose.** The 10-year Treasury yield climbed to about 4.96% from 4.91% after September job openings (JOLTS) came in above expectations.
- **Anthropic's compute deals.** An amended Anthropic IPO filing detailed long-term spending commitments with [[GOOGL]], [[AMZN]] (AWS) and [[MSFT]].

## Notable company news

- **AMD ([[AMD]])** reported Q3 revenue of about $13.4B (+45%) and adjusted EPS of $2.09 after the close, both above forecasts, with data-center sales nearly doubling; it guided Q4 revenue to about $17B versus roughly $16.3B expected. Shares rose about 3.7% in the session and climbed after hours.
- **Amgen ([[AMGN]])** posted Q3 revenue of about $10.4B (+8%) and EPS of $6.45, above estimates, and raised its 2026 revenue range; shares slipped after hours as Prolia/XGEVA sales fell sharply.
- **Palantir ([[PLTR]])** fell about 1.6% despite Monday's beat-and-raise, as slower growth in new contract value and valuation worries weighed; Morgan Stanley and Piper Sandler lifted targets.
- **Marvell ([[MRVL]])** was downgraded by BofA and TD Cowen over the Lumentum deal, with targets cut sharply.
- **Meta ([[META]])** began marketing a bond sale of at least $20B, including its first euro-denominated debt.
- **Accenture ([[ACN]])** fell about 4% and **Walmart ([[WMT]])** about 2% on valuation worries; DZ Bank cut [[MRK]] and [[CVX]] to Hold.
- **Other movers (not in the vault):** DuPont rose about 8.6% and Revvity about 7.8%; Shopify fell about 9% on slower merchandise-volume growth.
