# Market Scan: Fri, Nov 6, 2026

> [!summary] The day in one line
> Stocks steadied after Thursday's AI rout: chip stocks won back roughly a third of their losses and lifted the Nasdaq 1.1%, while the S&P 500 edged up 0.3% and the Dow slipped 0.5%; a much stronger-than-expected October jobs report pushed yields up and kept a December Fed hike in play, and the S&P 500 still logged its worst week since June.

## What moved the market

- **Chips bounced, partly.** Eight large chip stocks recovered an average of about 36% of Thursday's losses, per StockTitan: [[AMD]] +11.0%, [[INTC]] +7.7%, [[MRVL]] +5.7%, [[QCOM]] +5.5%, [[MU]] +5.1%, [[NVDA]] +2.8% and [[AVGO]] only +0.7%. [[TSM]] ADRs gained about 7.5% even as Taiwan's market fell 4.5% and regulators tightened short-selling rules.
- **Chip equipment rallied.** [[AMAT]] closed up about 7.9% and [[ASML]] about 5% as Lam Research (not in the vault) jumped about 8% after a $25B buyback.
- **Jobs blew past forecasts.** October payrolls rose 165,000 against roughly 60,000–70,000 expected, and unemployment dipped to 4.1%; yields rose and traders kept odds of a December Fed hike above 65%.
- **Weekly damage.** Tech (XLK) fell about 7% on the week and energy (XLE) about 11% as Brent slipped below $100 on Iran de-escalation hopes.
- **Overreaction or warning?** Yardeni Research called the selloff an overreaction and kept its 7,900 year-end S&P 500 target; worries about post-midterm data-center limits and AI spending remained the main overhang.

## Notable company news

- **Nvidia ([[NVDA]])**: three senior House Republicans asked Jensen Huang to a closed-door briefing on Nov 18 about reported contacts with a Singapore-based executive alleged to have passed information to China.
- **AMD ([[AMD]])**: JPMorgan raised its target to $575 while Goldman trimmed its target to $675 but kept a Buy.
- **Microsoft ([[MSFT]])** is winding down a 2023 deal to rent Oracle-built AI servers for Bing, per Bloomberg; Oracle slipped about 3%.
- **Meta ([[META]])** closed a $15B five-part bond sale; shares rose about 2.7%.
- **Tesla ([[TSLA]])** rebounded about 3–4% after Elon Musk said Cybertruck output in Texas will wind down around mid-2027 to make room for Cybercab.
- **Palo Alto ([[PANW]])** rose about 3.9%, with reports it is also in talks to buy Beacon Security; [[PLTR]] bounced about 4.6% but was still down about 14.5% for the week.
- **Lilly ([[LLY]])** will let Hims & Hers sell Zepbound and Foundayo directly; Freedom Broker cut [[AMGN]] to Hold on valuation.
- **Walmart ([[WMT]])** fell nearly 3% on consumer and margin worries ahead of its Nov 19 report, while [[KO]] rose about 3% in a beverage-stock rally.
- **Energy:** Wells Fargo trimmed its [[COP]] target to $185; Freedom Broker raised [[CVX]] to Hold.
- **Other movers (not in the vault):** Gogo +71.5%, JFrog +35%, IREN +20% on AI cloud revenue, Airbnb +11.7%; DraftKings -21%, Axon -18%, Atlassian -16%.
