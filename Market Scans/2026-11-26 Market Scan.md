> [!summary] The day in one line

## What moved the market

- **Holiday bounce in Asia and Europe.** After Wednesday's steep morning selloff on Wall Street mostly reversed by the close, AI and chip names led overseas gains. Japan's Kioxia jumped 12.6% and SoftBank 7.3%; Korea's Kospi managed only a small gain after a 6% drop the day before.
- **Tariff details: chip tools spared.** The U.S. Trade Representative's formal Section 301 proposals for 14 economies include tariffs of up to 15% on Taiwan, South Korea and Japan and 15% on the EU, but the EU list leaves out chipmaking equipment. That helped [[ASML]] rise about 3.2% in Amsterdam and Taiwan's market rebound, though [[TSM]] slipped 0.2% in Taipei as foreign investors kept selling. China's commerce ministry objected to the probe. Comments are due Jan 5 and hearings start Jan 25.
- **Oil firmed on Gulf supply risk.** Yemen's Houthis claimed an attack on Saudi Arabia's Ras Tanura facility (Saudi officials said drones were intercepted), and there were reports of a halt to loadings there and of vessel incidents near Hormuz. Reuters had Brent dipping earlier in London trading, with one broker saying oversupply worries should cap gains. OPEC+ meets Sunday ([[CVX]], [[XOM]], [[COP]]).
- **Credit-rating watch on Nvidia.** S&P Global put [[NVDA]]'s AA rating on watch for a possible downgrade, citing governance concerns after the CEO turmoil; Moody's kept its rating stable.

## Notable company news

- **[[MU]]:** UBS kept Buy but cut its target to $1,100 from $1,625; Micron confirmed it reports on Dec 17. Asian memory peers rallied overnight.
- **[[ADI]]:** More post-earnings target trims — Citigroup and Needham both to $400, both keeping Buy ratings.
- **[[MRVL]]:** Oppenheimer kept Outperform but lowered its target to $325 ahead of Tuesday's report.
- **[[TXN]]:** Closed about 4% higher on Wednesday, reversing a morning drop.
- **[[AMZN]]:** Closed -3.1% Wednesday at $244.43 after Bernstein flagged weaker cloud web-traffic data; [[META]] closed +3.4% at $609.89 on reports of talks with Amazon over its shopping agent.
- **[[TSLA]]:** Closed -1.2% at $380.14 Wednesday after recovering most of an early drop; October retail sales in China were reported down about 66% from a year earlier.
- **[[AAPL]]:** Counterpoint data showed iPhone sales in China fell 8% over the Double 11 shopping festival, better than the overall phone market's 17% drop.
- **[[PANW]]:** Called a former vice president's accounting-fraud lawsuit meritless; shares fell about 5% in after-hours trading Wednesday.
- **[[PLTR]]:** Poland's defense ministry is set to sign an AI decision-support software contract with Palantir's Polish unit on Nov 27.
- **[[UNH]]:** Bernstein reiterated Buy with a $500 target (per Ad-hoc News); German trading pointed slightly higher.
- **[[PEP]]** and **[[KO]]:** India's food regulator ordered tougher checks on packaged water, covering both companies' brands.
