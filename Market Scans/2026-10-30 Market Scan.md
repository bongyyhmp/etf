# Market Scan: Fri, Oct 30, 2026

> [!summary] The day in one line
> Intel's roughly 29% collapse on a weak outlook set off a chip rout that erased about $1 trillion of semiconductor value, and Amazon's results deepened doubts about AI spending; the Nasdaq fell nearly 3% to close out October, while energy stocks hit records after Exxon and Chevron beat.

## What moved the market

- **Intel's worst day in decades.** [[INTC]] closed down about 29% at roughly $65 after guiding Q4 revenue to $15–16B, citing a PC slump and memory costs; BofA and Roth downgraded it to Neutral.
- **A $1 trillion chip wipeout.** The semiconductor index fell roughly 7%–9%. [[AMD]] and [[QCOM]] dropped about 12% intraday, [[AMAT]] and [[ASML]] about 8%, [[MU]] and [[AVGO]] about 6%–7%, [[NVDA]] about 5%, and [[MRVL]] another ~10%. [[SOXX]] and [[SMH]] fell about 8%.
- **AI-spending doubts grew.** After misses from Alphabet and Amazon, investors questioned whether Big Tech's capex will pay off; Amazon's trailing free cash flow turned deeply negative. Nvidia also faced "circular financing" worries after a report it may guarantee loans for an Anthropic data center.
- **Oil and rates.** WTI crude rose about 2% to near $104 on Strait of Hormuz tensions; the 10-year Treasury yield eased to about 4.92% as investors sought safety, even as Fed officials floated the possibility of further hikes.
- **Crypto hit.** A reported $6.3B hack at Binance pushed Bitcoin down about 5% to near $76,600.

## Notable company news

- **Amazon ([[AMZN]])** pared an early ~5% drop to finish down about 2% after AWS grew 39%; analysts split, with UBS and Mizuho raising targets and BofA, Wells Fargo and others cutting.
- **Exxon ([[XOM]])** posted adjusted Q3 EPS of $4.23, above estimates, on strong refining, and plans about $20B of buybacks in 2027; shares neared a record.
- **Chevron ([[CVX]])** beat with adjusted EPS of $5.28 and rose about 2% near its 1-year high; [[COP]] hit an all-time high ahead of its Nov 5 report.
- **AbbVie ([[ABBV]])** beat on Q3 sales and EPS, raised full-year guidance and lifted its dividend about 5%; shares rose about 1.7%.
- **Health care held up:** [[LLY]] rose about 3% to a record as several brokers raised targets; [[MRK]] gained about 2.6%; [[AMGN]] and [[JNJ]] touched new highs.
- **Alphabet ([[GOOGL]])** slipped another ~2% and Wells Fargo cut its target; [[META]] fell about 2%.
- **Other movers (not in the vault):** Reddit fell about 24%–29% on a weak forecast, Roblox about 16% and Twilio about 17%; Dell dropped on PC and server worries; Regeneron rose about 11% after raising its outlook.
