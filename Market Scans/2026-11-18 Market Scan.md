# Market Scan: Wed, Nov 18, 2026

> [!summary] The day in one line

## What moved the market

- **Nvidia's beat didn't stick.** [[NVDA]] erased its after-hours gain and closed down about 2.9% near $151, its fifth straight loss, after board chair Harvey Jones ruled out Jensen Huang's return on the call and the company said its 10-Q filing would be delayed by an investigation. Several brokers cut targets again (Jefferies, Morgan Stanley, Wells Fargo, Piper Sandler, UBS).
- **Rotation within chips.** Money moved toward Nvidia alternatives: [[AMD]] rose about 5.3% and [[AVGO]] 3.6%, while [[MU]] (-1.0%), [[MRVL]] (-2.2%) and [[QCOM]] (about -3% to -4%) fell. European chip names including [[ASML]] gained.
- **Insurers extend their slide.** Managed-care stocks fell for a second day on CMS's proposed anti-fraud audit rule for Medicare Advantage: Humana -13%, [[UNH]] -10.2% (about 15% in two days) and Elevance -7.6%. Oppenheimer called the selloff an overreaction.
- **Oil and gold drop.** A larger-than-expected EIA crude build (3.4M barrels vs. about 58K expected) pushed Brent below $90 for the first time since late August, making energy the weakest sector; gold fell about 3.5%.
- **Macro.** Pending home sales fell 4.7% in October to the lowest level in records going back to 2001. Fed Governor Chris Waller said a December quarter-point hike should come with a signal of another in January; CME FedWatch showed about 69% odds of 50 basis points of hikes by end-January.

## Notable company news

- **Earnings:** [[PANW]] beat and raised after the close: fiscal Q1 revenue of $3.44B (+39%) vs. about $3.31B expected, adjusted EPS $1.05 vs. $0.97, and full-year revenue guidance lifted to $14.52–14.57B; it also agreed to buy AI-model startup Applied Compute for about $4.5B in stock. Shares had already jumped 9.9% in the session (the S&P 500's top gainer) and rose further after hours. Target and Lowe's (not in the vault) beat estimates.
- **Anthropic IPO:** priced at $83 a share, above the $70–78 range, per Reuters; trading on Nasdaq as ANT is expected Thursday. [[AMZN]] and [[GOOGL]] hold large stakes, and [[NVDA]], [[AMD]], [[AVGO]], [[MU]] and [[TSM]] are buyers in the private placement.
- **Megacaps:** [[AMZN]] +3.8% to $267.74, the Dow's No. 2 gainer; [[MSFT]] about +0.4% as JPMorgan and Piper Sandler reiterated Overweight after Ignite; [[AAPL]] about +0.2%, saying Tuesday's iCloud outage came from an internal update; [[META]] about -0.4% (Bloomberg: share plan for Zuckerberg paused during an investor suit); [[TSLA]] about +0.9% as its Miami robotaxi area expanded. [[INTC]] slipped about 1% despite an expanded Azure supply deal with Microsoft.
- **Health care:** [[MRK]] -3.9% after ending its nemtabrutinib program; [[AMGN]] -3.1% with no company news; [[LLY]] about -1.9%.
- **Energy:** [[CVX]] -4.7% (also ex-dividend), the Dow's No. 2 loser; [[XOM]] about -4%. Morgan Stanley raised its [[COP]] target to $160.
- **Other:** [[CSCO]] +2.2%; [[ACN]] -3.6% on IT-budget caution. Brokers trimmed [[HD]] targets after its report (Goldman $371, UBS $417, Telsey $400). Wolfe Research started [[KO]] at Outperform and [[PEP]] at Peer Perform. [[JPM]] about -1.2%.
- **Not in the vault:** Disney +4.6% (Dow's top gainer), Generac +9.9%, Akamai +8.3%, CoreWeave -4.1%.
