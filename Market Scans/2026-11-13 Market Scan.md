# Market Scan: Fri, Nov 13, 2026

> [!summary] The day in one line

## What moved the market

- **Chips sold off again.** Stocks opened higher and then turned as semiconductors slid: [[AMD]] was the S&P 500's worst performer (-8.75% to $534.06), [[AVGO]] fell about 5% to end a four-day run, and [[MU]], [[MRVL]], [[INTC]], [[QCOM]], [[KLAC]], [[TXN]] and [[ADI]] each lost roughly 3–5%. Michael Burry said he doubled his Nasdaq 100 puts and added puts on semiconductor ETFs.
- **Nvidia fallout continued.** [[NVDA]] fell 2.0% to $162.85, a 52-week low; HSBC cut it to Hold with a $170 target, the Senate Banking chair asked Jensen Huang to testify Dec 3, and interim CEO Colette Kress reportedly plans an all-hands on Nov 18 ahead of Tuesday's earnings.
- **Inflation and the Fed.** October producer prices rose 0.3% (a bit under forecasts) but 6.5% from a year earlier, the biggest annual gain since November 2022; traders treated a December 8–9 rate hike as close to certain and the 10-year yield rose further above 5%.
- **Defensives led.** Oil & gas, financials and healthcare were the strongest sectors; tech and industrials lagged. Breadth was slightly negative on both the NYSE and Nasdaq.

## Notable company news

- **Beat-and-raise, then sold:** [[CSCO]] fell 6.2% to $95.64, the Dow's worst, as slowing product orders and margin pressure overshadowed its raised outlook, with Rosenblatt, Piper Sandler, Citi, Wells Fargo and Barclays all trimming targets. [[AMAT]] dropped about 4% as at least 10 firms cut targets (mostly keeping Buy ratings) and Summit Insights downgraded to Hold.
- **Megacaps:** [[META]] rose 2.9% and [[AAPL]] 0.4%; [[MSFT]] edged up 0.2% while recovering from a large Microsoft 365 outage that began Thursday; [[GOOGL]] slipped about 1% after raising about $3.8B in its first Asian-currency bond sale; [[AMZN]] -0.3% and [[TSLA]] -1.8% after a five-day winning streak.
- **Security:** [[PANW]] traded higher after KeyBanc upgraded it to Overweight ($380 target) ahead of Wednesday's report.
- **Dow leaders:** [[KO]] gained 1.9% to an all-time high of $99.25; [[CVX]] rose 1.6% to $219.89 and [[WMT]] 1.5% to $128.09 (Wells Fargo lifted its target to $135). Barclays raised targets on [[XOM]], [[CVX]] and [[COP]]; [[PG]] hit a 52-week high.
- **Healthcare:** An EU advisory panel backed expanding [[MRK]]'s RSV antibody Enflonsia to a second season; a report said China Merchants Securities cut [[LLY]] to Hold (details unconfirmed).
- **Financials:** [[JPM]]'s quarterly 13F filing showed larger holdings of a bitcoin ETF and Strategy shares.
- **Not in the vault:** Dell fell about 7% and CoreWeave about 6%; Cerebras jumped about 11% after record quarterly revenue; Modine soared about 28% on a Bloomberg report of Samsung takeover talks; Charter led the S&P 500 (+4.3%).
