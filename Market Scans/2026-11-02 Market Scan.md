# Market Scan: Mon, Nov 2, 2026

> [!summary] The day in one line
> Stocks faded an early bounce as chipmakers kept sliding and the Nasdaq fell about 1%, while the Dow edged higher on a rotation into consumer and financial names; oil plunged nearly 8% on hopes of US–Iran diplomacy, and Apple and Palantir both beat after the close.

## What moved the market

- **Chip selling continued.** Semiconductors led the Nasdaq lower for a second session after Friday's Intel-driven rout; [[MRVL]] fell about 5% after confirming its roughly $115B all-stock deal for Lumentum, which dropped about 10%.
- **Oil tumbled.** WTI crude fell about 7.7% to near $96 and Brent dropped below $105 on reports that Washington wants to give diplomacy with Tehran another try. Energy stocks slid: [[XOM]] lost about 3.9% and [[CVX]] about 3.2% from near-record levels, while airlines such as United jumped.
- **Rotation into defensives and cyclicals.** The Dow gained as consumer, financial and staples stocks rose: [[WMT]] up about 3.4%, [[KO]] about 2% to a 52-week high, [[PG]] about 1.5% and [[BRK-B]] about 2%.
- **Factory data beat.** The October ISM manufacturing index rose to 55.5 from 54.5, above the 54.6 forecast and a 10th straight month of expansion; its prices gauge eased but stayed high.
- **Rates and the Fed.** The 10-year Treasury yield slipped to about 4.89%. CME data showed traders pricing roughly a 76% chance of a quarter-point Fed hike in December.
- **AI funding in focus.** Oracle (not in the vault) fell about 5% after announcing about $20B of new bonds and stock to fund AI spending, while Alphabet's euro bond sale was received better.

## Notable company news

- **Apple ([[AAPL]])** beat fiscal Q4 forecasts after the close, with revenue of about $114.5B (+12%) and EPS of $2.10; iPhone sales rose 17% and Services hit a record, but Greater China fell short. Shares lost about 2% in the session and slipped after hours.
- **Palantir ([[PLTR]])** grew Q3 revenue 96% to about $2.31B, saw US commercial sales pass US government sales for the first time, and guided Q4 well above consensus; shares closed down about 3% and rose after hours.
- **Marvell ([[MRVL]])** set out a fiscal-2031 revenue goal of $70–90B alongside the Lumentum deal, which is expected to close in the first half of 2028.
- **Alphabet ([[GOOGL]])** sold about €6B of euro bonds in seven parts, with orders reportedly above €17B; shares ended roughly flat after an early gain.
- **Meta ([[META]])**: S&P moved its credit-rating outlook to negative, citing rising debt and AI capex.
- **Amazon ([[AMZN]])** rose about 2% after Phillip Securities upgraded it to Buy; **Tesla ([[TSLA]])** gained about 3% on a jump in Swedish registrations.
- **Analyst moves:** Bernstein cut its [[QCOM]] target ahead of earnings, citing memory costs; Cantor raised targets on [[LLY]] and [[MRK]]; several brokers lifted [[ABBV]] targets after its Q3 beat.
- **Other movers (not in the vault):** IDEXX jumped about 16% on a beat-and-raise, Monolithic Power about 8% and United Airlines about 7%; Datadog fell about 5% on cloud-spending worries.
