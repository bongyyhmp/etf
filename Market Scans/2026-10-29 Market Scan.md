# Market Scan: Thu, Oct 29, 2026

> [!summary] The day in one line
> Alphabet's roughly 12% plunge after its earnings miss dragged the Nasdaq down about 1.4%, while small caps rallied and Lilly and Merck beats lifted health care; after the bell, Intel sank on a weak outlook and Amazon slipped despite strong AWS growth.

## What moved the market

- **Alphabet's worst day in its public history.** [[GOOGL]] closed down about 12.6% after its EPS miss and faster AI-related cash burn, wiping out close to $500B of market value and reviving worries about AI spending.
- **Hot growth, hotter inflation.** Q3 GDP grew at a 3.4% annual rate (about 3.1% expected), while September PCE inflation rose to 3.6% year over year and core PCE to 3.1%, a day after the Fed's two hawkish dissents.
- **Chips cooled.** Samsung's profit miss and lighter-than-expected capex from Microsoft and Alphabet weighed on [[MU]] (about -5%), [[AMD]] and [[AVGO]]; [[MRVL]] fell about 11–13% on reports it is in advanced talks to buy Lumentum.
- **Software and security sold off.** ServiceNow (about -25%) and Fortinet (about -21%) dropped after their reports, pulling [[PANW]] down about 5%.
- **Yields eased, oil steady.** The 10-year Treasury yield slipped to about 4.99%; December crude settled near $102.91.

## Notable company news

- **Eli Lilly ([[LLY]])** beat estimates with Q3 revenue of $24.4B (+39%) and raised its 2026 revenue outlook to $91–92B; shares rose about 6%–7%.
- **Merck ([[MRK]])** topped forecasts with $18.1B in sales and $2.49 EPS and raised its profit outlook; shares gained about 4%–5%.
- **Microsoft ([[MSFT]])** gave back an early gain to trade lower after its Azure-led beat, as investors weighed a planned bond sale of at least $25B; several brokers raised targets.
- **Meta ([[META]])** rose about 3% midday, snapping a five-day losing streak.
- **Amazon ([[AMZN]])** reported AWS growth of 39% to $46.0B, but EPS of $1.89 missed and ad revenue fell short; shares fell as much as 10% after hours before paring losses.
- **Intel ([[INTC]])** beat on Q3 EPS ($0.52) but guided Q4 revenue to $15–16B, below estimates, citing memory costs; shares dropped about 16%–17% after hours.
- **Applied Materials ([[AMAT]])** joined the vault as the new #10 holding of [[XLK]] and [[VGT]], replacing Palo Alto Networks; it rose about 5% on Wednesday after agreeing to raise its stake in Besi.
- **Other movers (not in the vault):** ADT jumped about 34% on a buyout deal; Crocs rose about 25%; Carvana gained about 15%; Sprouts Farmers Market fell about 18%–22%; Caterpillar slid despite a profit beat.
