# Market Scan: Week Ending Fri, Oct 23, 2026

> [!summary] The week in one line
> A choppy week. Stocks rose through Wednesday, then fell Thursday and Friday on AI-spending worries and oil above $100. The S&P 500 and Nasdaq finished slightly down; the Dow finished slightly up.

## What moved the market

- **Thursday's sell-off.** The Nasdaq had its worst day since June. The causes were fresh worries about how much companies are spending on AI, plus higher oil prices.
- **Oil.** Crude is above $100 and still rising. That feeds inflation fears, which push bond yields up. On Friday, oil-sensitive consumer stocks took the hit while chip stocks steadied.
- **Breadth was weak.** On Friday, nearly three of every four S&P 500 stocks fell. Consumer staples and financials led the declines; energy was the standout gainer.
- **"Ex-AI" stocks are winning October.** An S&P 500 index that excludes AI stocks has outperformed the regular index this month, as worries about AI financing weigh on the biggest names.

## Notable company news

### Meta (META): beat, shares up
- Q3 revenue was **$64.6B, up 26%**, ahead of estimates.
- Reported EPS was $3.83, dragged down by a **$9.89B legal settlement** with state attorneys general over teen safety. Excluding that, EPS would have been about $7.40, well above consensus.
- Q4 revenue guidance is **$73–76B**. Full-year expense guidance was raised to include the settlement.
- Shares rose about 5% after hours.

### Netflix (NFLX): beat on profit, shares down
- Q3 revenue was **$12.87B (+12%)**, in line with estimates. EPS was $0.87, above estimates of about $0.82–0.83.
- It **cut its 2026 ad revenue forecast to ~$2.7B** from $3B, citing slower product rollouts.
- Q4 profit guidance came in below what analysts expected.
- Shares fell about 6% after hours.

### Tesla (TSLA): in line, shares up slightly
- Q3 revenue was **$28.05B**, flat year over year and in line with estimates.
- Deliveries were 486,532, well ahead of consensus but down 2.1% year over year. Profit fell.
- Shares rose about 1.7% after the report.

### IBM (IBM): beat, shares up
- Revenue was **$17.1B** vs. $16.8B expected. Adjusted EPS was $2.95 vs. $2.85 expected.
- Shares rose about 4% after hours.

### Also reported
- **Lam Research (LRCX)** and **Las Vegas Sands (LVS)** both disappointed investors. Details are not covered in this scan.

## Watch next week (Oct 26–30)

- **Fed meeting, Oct 27–28.** Rising oil and inflation worries make this one important.
- **Big Tech earnings:** Microsoft and Alphabet on Oct 28; Amazon and Intel on Oct 29. Watch their AI spending plans closely.
- About **one-third of the S&P 500** reports this week.

## Open questions

- Will AI spending plans from Microsoft, Alphabet, and Amazon calm or deepen this month's AI worries?
- Does oil stay above $100, and how does the Fed respond?
