# Market Scan: Fri, Nov 27, 2026 (Black Friday, half day)

> [!summary] The day in one line
> In a shortened post-Thanksgiving session, chip stocks led a broad rally — the Philadelphia chip index jumped 4.7% with all 30 members higher — lifting the Nasdaq 2.3% and the S&P 500 1.6% and capping a holiday week of roughly 5–7% gains, while the VIX fell 11% and Nvidia lagged on a report it paused some Southeast Asia server shipments.

## What moved the market

- **Chip rebound.** After last week's AI-trade rout, semiconductors kept recovering: [[MU]] rose about 7.6% as memory names rebounded (despite a TrendForce warning that DRAM price gains may fade through 2027), [[AMD]] about 4.6%, [[AVGO]] about 3.8%, and [[TSM]]'s ADR 3.2%. Equipment makers [[AMAT]] and [[KLAC]] got a lift from a Senate bill that would extend the CHIPS Act equipment tax credit to 2031; [[KLAC]] also drew an Evercore ISI upgrade. [[MPWR]], [[NXPI]], [[QCOM]], [[INTC]] and [[CSCO]] gained roughly 3–7%, and [[MRVL]] rose ahead of Tuesday's report.
- **Nvidia lagged.** [[NVDA]] rose only 0.8% to $150.49 after the FT reported it told server partners to halt shipments to Malaysia, Singapore, Thailand and Indonesia under new U.S. license rules; Jefferies (per Finwire) put $44–62B of next year's data-center revenue at risk. Bloomberg also reported Blackwell racks at a Shanghai site tied to Megaspeed's founder.
- **Holiday shopping.** Adobe counted a record $7.1B of U.S. online spending on Thanksgiving (+10.4%) and forecast $12.9B for Black Friday and $15.1B for Cyber Monday. [[AMZN]] rose about 2–4% (sources differ); [[WMT]] was little changed early.
- **Oil eased** on the day ahead of Sunday's OPEC+ meeting, where Reuters sources expect output targets for early 2027 to stay unchanged ([[XOM]], [[CVX]], [[COP]]).
- **Defensives lagged.** With money rotating back into tech, health-care names were mixed: [[ABBV]] slipped about 0.9% while [[AMGN]] rose about 1%.

## Notable company news

- **[[PLTR]]:** Poland signed a five-year, roughly $300M contract for Palantir's Maven Smart System battlefield software; shares closed +5.2% at $172.17.
- **[[TSLA]]:** Closed +5.2% at $400.00 after filing with Germany's antitrust regulator to buy robotics-machinery maker EMAG; an EU vote on its driver-assist software may come Dec 15.
- **[[AAPL]]:** Rose 0.8% to a record close of $358.54.
- **[[META]]:** Up about 3%; Dutch consumer and digital-rights groups asked an Amsterdam court to halt its smart-glasses sales over privacy.
- **[[GOOGL]]:** Up about 1.4%; the Los Angeles school district switched off Google's AI search summaries for students.
- **[[MSFT]]:** Rose with tech; Bill Ackman said Pershing Square's reported share sale was offset by call options.
- **[[ASML]]:** The Dutch prime minister plans to raise the proposed U.S. MATCH Act chip-export bill with President Trump at the G20, per Reuters.
- **[[INTC]]:** Court filings in a Delaware shareholder suit, reported by WIRED, say Commerce pushed Intel to hand over about 10% of its equity to release CHIPS grants; a hearing is set for Dec 10.
- **[[KO]]:** Completed the sale of a 75% stake in Coca-Cola Beverages Africa to Coca-Cola HBC.
- **[[PEP]]:** Zacks Research cut its rank to Strong Sell (reported Nov 26).
- **[[PANW]]:** Slipped early as Northland cut its earnings estimates after the ex-VP's accounting lawsuit.
- **[[JPM]]:** Reported to be reviewing some UK investment plans as the UK bank surcharge rises.
- **[[BRK-B]]:** Subsidiary deals included McLane buying Willow Run Foods.
