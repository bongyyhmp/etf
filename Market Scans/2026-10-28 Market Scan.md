# Market Scan: Wed, Oct 28, 2026

> [!summary] The day in one line
> Stocks finished slightly higher after the Fed held rates steady in a split 10–2 vote, with two officials pushing for a hike; after the bell, Microsoft rose on an Azure-driven beat while Alphabet sank on an earnings-per-share miss.

## What moved the market

- **Fed held, but hawks dissented.** The Fed kept its benchmark rate at 3.75%–4.00%. Governor Lisa Cook and Cleveland Fed President Beth Hammack voted for a quarter-point hike. Chair Kevin Warsh said one 2027 meeting will be set aside for strategy with no rate vote. Futures priced roughly an 85% chance of a hike at the Dec. 9 meeting.
- **Memory chips rallied on SK Hynix.** SK Hynix's record quarter (operating profit up more than sixfold) and a large shareholder-return plan lifted [[MU]], [[AMD]], [[NVDA]] and [[AVGO]] for a third day of chip gains.
- **Oracle surged.** Oracle (not in the vault) jumped about 11% after projecting fiscal 2030 revenue of at least $225B.
- **Oil and yields rose.** December WTI crude gained about 1.8% to roughly $101.67 after the Houthis claimed a strike on a major Saudi refinery (not confirmed by Saudi officials). The 10-year Treasury yield climbed to about 5.05%.

## Notable company news

- **Microsoft ([[MSFT]])** posted fiscal Q1 revenue of about $92.3B (+19%) and adjusted EPS of $4.89, both above estimates, with Azure up 48%. Shares rose about 4%–6% after hours, though commercial bookings fell and backlog was flat versus June.
- **Alphabet ([[GOOGL]])** grew Cloud revenue about 103%, but EPS of $2.12 missed forecasts near $3, partly due to an $8.7B loss on equity holdings; shares fell roughly 8% after hours. The Gemini app reached 1 billion users.
- **AbbVie ([[ABBV]])** asked the FDA to approve etentamig, a monthly multiple myeloma drug, citing a 74% response rate in its Phase 3 trial.
- **Eli Lilly ([[LLY]])** said it will combine its US and international units into "Lilly Global Markets" when Patrik Jonsson retires at year-end; it reports Thursday.
- **Accenture ([[ACN]])** CEO Julie Sweet bought about $3M of stock, per a new filing, after shares slid 4.4% on Tuesday.
- **Apple ([[AAPL]])** unveiled its first touchscreen MacBook Pro, starting at $2,899 and shipping Nov. 6.
- **Tesla ([[TSLA]])** gave back about 1.4% after a 4% gain Tuesday on SpaceX merger talk.
- **Other movers (not in the vault):** Vertiv jumped about 17% on surging orders and a higher forecast; Wendy's rose about 14% after confirming a Trian-led buyout proposal; Enphase fell about 17% on a weak Q4 outlook; F5 slid about 20% on slower growth guidance; Robinhood fell as crypto revenue dropped.
