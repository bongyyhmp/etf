## What moved the market

- **Anthropic IPO filing.** The AI company filed its public prospectus to list on the Nasdaq as "ANT". The filing showed revenue of about $16.3B in the first half of 2026, a very large accounting-driven loss, and heavy customer concentration. Chip stocks such as [[NVDA]], [[AVGO]] and [[MU]] rose on the news.
- **Meta's $95B equity plan.** [[META]] said it would raise up to $95B in stock to pay for AI buildout, starting with about $35B now. [[NVDA]], [[AMD]] and [[AVGO]] were reported to be weighing purchases of up to $5B of it. Meta shares fell about 2%.
- **Oil cooled slightly.** Crude slipped on hopes of easing US-Iran tensions, though WTI stayed near $101 and Brent above $110. The 10-year Treasury yield sat near 4.99%.
- **AI financing jitters linger.** SpaceX said it lined up $40B of debt to buy AI chips, while Elon Musk warned that many smaller "neocloud" data-center firms could fail; CoreWeave and peers fell.

## Notable company news

- **Tesla ([[TSLA]])** rose about 6% after Barclays upgraded it to Overweight with a $450 target, citing a possible combination with SpaceX.
- **Apple ([[AAPL]])** lagged, down about 1%, after Jefferies repeated its bearish view on December-quarter results.
- **Microsoft ([[MSFT]])** edged higher as several analysts raised targets ahead of Wednesday's report; it was also reported to be offering voluntary buyouts to tens of thousands of staff.
- **Alphabet ([[GOOGL]])** slipped about 1% ahead of its report.
- **Deals and other movers:** EQT agreed to buy Range Resources in an all-stock deal valued above $50B (RRC +16%); Fair Isaac fell on analyst downgrades; Brazilian stocks XP and Nu sank after Brazil's presidential runoff.

- **Wed Oct 28:** Fed decision. Markets mostly expect a hold, with focus on whether it signals more hikes. [[MSFT]] and [[GOOGL]] report after the close.
