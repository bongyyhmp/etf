# Market Scan: Wed, Nov 11, 2026

> [!summary] The day in one line

## What moved the market

- **CoreWeave reignited AI stocks.** The AI cloud provider jumped about 24% after third-quarter revenue rose 168% to $3.7B and it raised its full-year revenue outlook to $13–13.3B; it also expects about $9B of customer prepayments over the next three months and said it won't add more expensive debt through 2027, easing financing worries that had weighed on the AI trade.
- **Chips led the rebound.** The Philadelphia Semiconductor Index rose about 5.7%. [[MU]] closed up 11.9% at $811.30 as Morgan Stanley's Joseph Moore said the memory pullback improves the risk-reward; [[INTC]] (+6.5%), [[MRVL]] (+6.4%), [[AVGO]] (+6.0%), [[AMD]] (+3.6%) and [[TSM]] ADRs (+3.2%) also gained. Chip-equipment names ran ahead of Thursday's [[AMAT]] report: [[KLAC]] rose about 7%, AMAT about 4–8% depending on the source, and [[ASML]] about 3%; analog chipmakers [[ADI]] (~+5%) and [[TXN]] (~+3.7%) rallied for a second day.
- **Nvidia bounced despite a downgrade.** [[NVDA]] closed up 2.92% at $173.73, ending a two-day slide tied to its CEO controversy, even as Baird cut it to Neutral (target $200) and Morgan Stanley dropped it as a top pick under a stop-loss rule while keeping Overweight; the stock is still down roughly 12% this month.
- **Rate-hike odds stayed high.** Coverage said a December Fed hike now looks nearly fully priced after Tuesday's hot CPI, with markets weighing the chance of more than one move; Treasuries didn't trade because of the holiday.

## Notable company news

- **Apple ([[AAPL]])** hit another record close near $355, a market value of about $5.16T, and China Renaissance upgraded it to Buy with a Street-high $425 target.
- **Megacap split:** [[MSFT]] fell 2.26% to $521.13, with Benzinga pointing to a proposed US ban on Chinese-made data-center optical transceivers and competition from Anthropic's new office tools; [[AMZN]] fell 1.74% and [[META]] 1.97%, while [[GOOGL]] rose 2.11%. Reuters calculated that Amazon's and Google's Anthropic stakes would be worth about $411B combined at the top of the IPO range. Bloomberg reported Meta infrastructure chief Santosh Janardhan will hand his team to ex-AWS executive Dave Brown in early 2027.
- **Tesla ([[TSLA]])** rose 2.91% to $390.84 after Musk said Cybertruck production will run through the end of summer 2027.
- **Earnings previews:** [[CSCO]] rose about 3.4% and [[PANW]] about 3.8% ahead of their reports; options imply roughly 8–9% swings for Cisco and Applied Materials on Thursday.
- **Retail and banks:** BofA raised its [[WMT]] target to $139 from $126 ahead of the Nov 19 report; [[HD]] drifted lower before its Nov 17 report; [[JPM]] is leading retail marketing and allocation for Anthropic's IPO.
- **Healthcare:** [[AMGN]] presented full Phase 3 dazodalibep data in Sjögren's disease at ACR — both doses hit the main goal, but only the higher dose improved dryness and no filing timeline was given; [[LLY]] presented psoriatic arthritis data for Taltz plus Zepbound.
- **Energy:** Oil edged up on doubts about a quick U.S.–Iran resolution after two down days; Freedom Broker cut [[COP]] to Hold from Buy on valuation. [[XOM]] and [[CVX]] remain below their late-October highs.
- **Other movers (not in the vault):** Nebius and Bloom Energy jumped about 15–16%; Lululemon fell on slowing revenue; Airbnb dipped on reports that OpenAI bought an AI travel-planning startup; bitcoin rose about 1% to near $74,500.
